The Icy Veins 12.1 recommendations below supersede the former gearing and rotation summary. Character-specific simulations and item level take priority whenever the guide calls out close or fluid stats.

**Talent and Hero Tree recommendation**
- Druid of the Claw is strong for single-target and Incarnation pulls; Elune's Chosen excels in multi-target through Lunar Beam and self-healing. Both remain viable.

**Stat priority**
- Survivability: item level > Haste > Versatility > Mastery > Critical Strike. Damage: Haste > Versatility > Critical Strike > Mastery. Higher item level usually wins.

**Gems**
- Indecipherable Eversong Diamond, then Flawless Versatile Peridot with Haste/Versatility balance.

**Enchants and weapon augment**
- Berserker's Rage weapon enchant and Thalassian Phoenix Oil; Akil'zon's Swiftness; Empowered Blessing of Speed; Mark of the Worldsoul or Rootwarden's Binding; Forest Hunter's or Blood Knight's Armor Kit; Farstrider's Hunt; Silvermoon's Alacrity.

**Consumables**
- Blood Knights flask, Light's Potential, Harandar/Silvermoon feast or Royal Roast, health potion/Healthstone and Void-Touched Augment Rune.

**Rotation and audit focus**
- Generate and spend Rage on Ironfur while keeping the defensive buff active, maintain Moonfire/Thrash and Tooth and Claw effects, and plan Incarnation, Barkskin and Survival Instincts around the damage profile.
