Source verification date: **2026-09-17**. Current guide pages: [guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/restoration-druid-pve-healing-guide) · [talents](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/restoration-druid-pve-healing-spec-builds-talents) · [stats](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/restoration-druid-pve-healing-stat-priority) · [enchants](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/restoration-druid-pve-healing-gems-enchants-consumables) · [rotation](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/restoration-druid-pve-healing-rotation-cooldowns-abilities).

The Icy Veins 12.1 recommendations below supersede the former gearing and rotation summary. Character-specific simulations and item level take priority whenever the guide calls out close or fluid stats.

**Talent and Hero Tree recommendation**
- Wildstalker is the default for Mythic+ and small groups and is competitive in raid; Keeper of the Grove offers a burst-healing alternative.

**Stat priority**
- Raid: Intellect > Haste > Mastery > Versatility > Critical Strike. Dungeon gear is more profile-dependent; item level and encounter sims decide close upgrades.

**Gems**
- Indecipherable Eversong Diamond, then Flawless Masterful Peridot for Haste/Mastery.

**Enchants and weapon augment**
- Thalassian Phoenix Oil; Empowered Hex of Leeching; Silvermoon's Mending; Mark of the Worldsoul; Arcanoweave Spellthread; Shaladrassil's Roots; +29 Mastery rings; Acuity of the Ren'dorei weapon enchant.

**Consumables**
- Blood Knights flask for raid, Magisters for Mythic+ when throughput stats favor it; Potion of Recklessness rather than mana potions by default; Concentrated Silvermoon Health Potion; Silvermoon/Quel'Dorei/Harandar feast; Void-Touched Augment Rune.

**Rotation and audit focus**
- Maintain Lifebloom and Photosynthesis coverage, Rejuvenation for Abundance, Efflorescence and Swiftmend on cooldown, then Wild Growth for real group damage. Extend HoTs with Tranquility/Convoke and reserve Ironbark/Innervate for planned events.
