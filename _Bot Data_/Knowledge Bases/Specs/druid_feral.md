
## Feral Druid Knowledge Base (Patch 12.1) - RAID/M+ FOCUS

### Current 12.1 Source Snapshot
Source verification date: **2026-09-17**. Current guide pages: [guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/feral-druid-pve-dps-guide) · [talents](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/feral-druid-pve-dps-spec-builds-talents) · [stats](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/feral-druid-pve-dps-stat-priority) · [enchants](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/feral-druid-pve-dps-gems-enchants-consumables) · [rotation](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/feral-druid-pve-dps-rotation-cooldowns-abilities).

The Icy Veins 12.1 recommendations below supersede the former gearing and rotation summary. Character-specific simulations and item level take priority whenever the guide calls out close or fluid stats.

**Talent and Hero Tree recommendation**
- Wildstalker and Druid of the Claw are very close in raid single target; Wildstalker transitions more easily to cleave, while Druid of the Claw edges Mythic+ through defense and lower execution demand.

**Stat priority**
- Prioritize Agility and item level, then use sims to balance the close secondary stats for the chosen Hero Tree and target profile.

**Gems**
- Indecipherable Eversong Diamond, then Flawless Quick Amethyst; use Powerful Eversong Diamond plus color activation when socket count supports it.

**Enchants and weapon augment**
- Rite of Hash'ey and Thalassian Phoenix Oil; Empowered Rune of Avoidance; Amirdrassil's Grace; Mark of the Worldsoul; Forest Hunter's Armor Kit; Lynx's Dexterity; Eyes of the Eagle.

**Consumables**
- Magisters for Claw or Blood Knights for Wildstalker when that best fits the stat profile; Potion of Recklessness; current feast/personal food and Void-Touched Augment Rune.

**Rotation and audit focus**
- Snapshot Tiger's Fury with bleeds and buffs, keep Rip/Rake uptime, spend resources without clipping strong bleeds, and align Berserk, Convoke and Ashamane's Guidance with the selected tree.
