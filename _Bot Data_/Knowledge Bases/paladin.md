#### Current 12.1 Source Snapshot
Source verification date: **2026-09-17**. The Icy Veins 12.1 recommendations below supersede the former gearing and rotation summary. Character-specific simulations and item level take priority whenever the guide calls out close or fluid stats.

**Talent and Hero Tree recommendation**
- Herald of the Sun is the raid default and a reliable Mythic+ choice; Lightsmith is a viable Mythic+ damage/alternative build.

**Stat priority**
- Mythic+: Item Level > Mastery > Haste > Critical Strike > Versatility. Raid and Hero Tree-specific weights should be simmed.

**Gems**
- Telluric or Indecipherable Eversong Diamond; fill remaining sockets with the Haste/Mastery or Versatility gem that matches the build.

**Enchants and weapon augment**
- Acuity of the Ren'dorei; Empowered Hex of Leeching; Silvermoon's Mending; Mark of the Magister; Arcanoweave Spellthread; Shaladrassil's Roots; +29 Mastery or Silvermoon's Alacrity rings.

**Consumables**
- Magisters flask for healing; Blood Knights or Shattered Sun in Mythic+ when stats favor it; Lightfused Mana Potion when mana matters, otherwise Potion of Recklessness; health potion, Hearty Royal Roast/Silvermoon Parade and Void-Touched Augment Rune.

**Rotation and audit focus**
- Do not waste Holy Power. Use Divine Toll on cooldown, Beacon of Virtue for group damage, Infusion of Light with Flash of Light/Judgment, Holy Shock on cooldown, and spend Holy Power on Eternal Flame or Light of Dawn based on the damage pattern.

The Icy Veins 12.1 recommendations below supersede the former gearing and rotation summary. Character-specific simulations and item level take priority whenever the guide calls out close or fluid stats.

**Talent and Hero Tree recommendation**
- Templar is the current raid and Mythic+ recommendation; Lightsmith is the simpler alternative. Templar's Divine Exaction/Divine Toll cycle is tuned for Season 2.

**Stat priority**
- Defensive: Haste > Versatility > Mastery > Critical Strike. Offensive: Haste > Critical Strike > Versatility > Mastery. Item level remains the first filter.

**Gems**
- Indecipherable Eversong Diamond, then Flawless Masterful Peridot.

**Enchants and weapon augment**
- Thalassian Phoenix Oil for Templar or Rite of Sanctification for Lightsmith; Empowered Blessing of Speed; Akil'zon's Swiftness; Mark of the Worldsoul; Blood Knight's Armor Kit; Farstrider's Hunt; Silvermoon's Tenacity; Acuity of the Ren'dorei.

**Consumables**
- Shattered Sun flask; Light's Potential; health potion; Champion's Bento or Harandar/Blooming feast; Void-Touched Augment Rune.

**Rotation and audit focus**
- Keep Consecration active, generate Holy Power for Shield of the Righteous, and align Avenging Wrath, Divine Toll, Shield of the Righteous and Avenger's Shield. Plan Active Mitigation before damage rather than after the hit.

**Stat priority**

**Enchants and weapon augment**

**Consumables**
- Magisters flask; Potion of Recklessness or Light's Potential by profile; current feast/personal food and Void-Touched Augment Rune.

**Rotation and audit focus**
- Open with Blade of Justice, Avenging Wrath, potion/trinkets, Execution Sentence, Wake of Ashes and Final Verdict/Divine Storm, then Divine Toll. Keep Holy Power spenders flowing and adapt the spender to single target versus AoE.
