#### 2. Stat Priority Split
*   **Herald Focus:** Intellect > Mastery > Haste > Critical Strike > Versatility.

---

#### 3. Hero Talents Deep Dive

##### **Herald of the Sun**
*   **Core Focus:** **Dawnlight** & **Eternal Flame**.
*   **Gameplay Effect:** Introduces *Dawnlight* as a massive raid-wide HoT. Requires precise management of *Holy Shock* to spread radiance damage/healing.
*   **Why Pick?** Superior for raid-wide stabilization and overall HPS in 12.0.5.

##### **Lightsmith**
*   **Core Mechanic:** **Holy Armaments** & **Avenging Crusader**.
*   **Gameplay Effect:** Increases the impact of your utility weapons (*Bulwark/Sacred Weapon*). Focuses on high-intensity spot healing windows.
*   **Why Pick?** Superior for priority target protection and encounters requiring periodic high-burst utility.

---

#### 4. Deep Dive: Raid Rotation
Focus: **Holy Power Management** and **Glimmer Maintenance**.

##### **A. The Holy Power Audit (The "Never Cap" Rule)**
1.  **Rule:** Spend Holy Power immediately at **5 stacks**.
2.  **Audit Flag:** Casting **Holy Shock**, **Judgment**, or **Crusader Strike** while at 5 Holy Power is a catastrophic resource failure.

##### **B. Primary Priority List**
1.  **Holy Shock:** Top priority. Use on cooldown to generate Holy Power and maintain *Glimmer of Light*.
2.  **Divine Toll:** Use to instantly apply Glimmer to 5 targets and generate 5 Holy Power.
3.  **Light of Dawn:** Primary Holy Power spender for raid healing.
4.  **Word of Glory / Eternal Flame:** Secondary spender for priority triage.
5.  **Judgment:** Use to generate Holy Power and trigger *Judgment of Light*.
6.  **Audit Flag:** Letting **Glimmer of Light** fall off all targets during combat is a baseline loss error.

---

#### 5. Major Cooldowns Breakdown (Strict Whitelist)
*   **Avenging Wrath:** 2-minute CD. Increases all healing and damage.
*   **Aura Mastery:** 3-minute CD. Major raid-wide damage reduction.
*   **Divine Toll:** 1-minute CD. High-impact burst healing and resource generator.
*   **Blessing of Sacrifice:** Primary external for tanks or low-health allies.
*   **Lay on Hands:** Ultimate emergency heal.

#### 3. Hero Talents Deep Dive

##### **Templar**
*   **Core Focus:** **Hammer of Light** & **Lights Guidance**.
*   **Gameplay Effect:** Increases the damage and mitigation of your *Wake of Ashes* window. Replaces *Hammer of Wrath* with *Hammer of Light* during specific procs.
*   **Why Pick?** Superior for single-target boss encounters and offensive pressure in 12.0.5.

##### **Lightsmith**
*   **Core Mechanic:** **Holy Armaments** & **Bulwark**.
*   **Gameplay Effect:** Focuses on high-intensity physical mitigation through the use of *Bulwark* weapons.
*   **Why Pick?** Superior for pure safety and physical-heavy encounters.

---

#### 4. Deep Dive: Raid Rotation
Focus: **Consecration Uptime** and **Active Mitigation**.

##### **A. The Consecration Audit (The 100% Rule)**
1.  **Rule:** Maintain **100% uptime** on Consecration while actively tanking.
2.  **Audit Flag:** Tanking any target while outside the Consecration area is a catastrophic defensive failure.

##### **B. Mitigation & Holy Power**
1.  **Shield of the Righteous:** Primary Holy Power spender. Maintain active mitigation at all times.
2.  **Word of Glory:** Use only with **Shining Light** procs for self-healing, or if at < 50% HP.
3.  **Audit Flag:** Casting *Word of Glory* without a Shining Light proc when *Shield of the Righteous* mitigation is falling off is an efficiency error.

##### **C. Priority List**
1.  **Judgment:** Top priority generator. Generates Holy Power and reduces CD of *Shield of the Righteous*.
2.  **Avenger's Shield:** Generates high threat and absorbs damage (via *Bulwark of Order*).
3.  **Hammer of Wrath:** Use on cooldown during Avenging Wrath or on targets < 20% HP.
4.  **Hammer of the Righteous / Blessed Hammer:** Filler generator.

---

#### 5. Major Cooldowns Breakdown (Strict Whitelist)
*   **Guardian of Ancient Kings:** 5-minute CD. 50% Damage Reduction. Use for predictable lethal hits.
*   **Ardent Defender:** 2-minute CD. 20% Damage Reduction and prevents death once.
*   **Avenging Wrath:** 2-minute CD. Increases all damage and healing.
*   **Divine Toll:** 1-minute CD. Instant high threat and resource generator.
*   **Eye of Tyr:** 1-minute CD. Reduces damage taken from nearby enemies.

#### 3. Hero Talents Deep Dive

##### **Templar**
*   **Core Focus:** **Hammer of Light** & **Shake the Heavens**.
*   **Gameplay Effect:** Introduces a massive burst window triggered by *Wake of Ashes*. Replaces *Hammer of Wrath* with *Hammer of Light* (a heavy AoE/ST spender).
*   **Why Pick?** Superior for pure single-target burst and priority target damage in 12.0.5.

##### **Herald of the Sun**
*   **Core Mechanic:** **Dawnlight** & **Solar Ray**.
*   **Gameplay Effect:** Adds a massive radiance DoT to your *Wake of Ashes* and *Final Verdict*. Focuses on consistent throughput and area damage.
*   **Why Pick?** Superior for consistent multi-target cleave and target-rich encounters.

---

#### 4. Deep Dive: Raid Rotation
Focus: **Execution Sentence Window** and **Holy Power Efficiency**.

##### **A. The Burst Audit (The Execution Window)**
1.  **Execution Sentence:** Use on cooldown (ensure you have at least 3 Holy Power available).
2.  **Wake of Ashes:** Cast immediately after Execution Sentence.
3.  **Hammer of Light (Templar):** Use all available procs within the Execution window.
4.  **Final Verdict:** Primary Holy Power spender. 
5.  **Audit Flag:** Casting **Wake of Ashes** BEFORE **Execution Sentence** is a major throughput error.

##### **B. Holy Power Management**
1.  **Final Verdict:** Spend at **4-5 Holy Power**.
2.  **Blade of Justice:** Use on cooldown (triggered by *Art of War*).
3.  **Judgment:** Use on cooldown (applies *Judgment of Light*).
4.  **Hammer of Wrath:** Use on cooldown (during Avenging Wrath or on targets < 20% HP).
5.  **Audit Flag:** Casting *Blade of Justice* or *Judgment* when at **5 Holy Power** is a catastrophic resource waste.

---

#### 5. Major Cooldowns Breakdown (Strict Whitelist)
*   **Avenging Wrath (Crusade):** 2-minute CD. Increases damage and Haste.
*   **Wake of Ashes:** 45s CD. Generates 3 Holy Power and triggers Hero Talent procs.
*   **Execution Sentence:** 30s CD. Increases damage taken by the target.
*   **Divine Toll:** 1-minute CD. Instant high threat/damage and resource generator.
*   **Shield of Vengeance:** Primary defensive absorb.
