#### 2. Stat Priority
*   **General Priority:** Intellect > Mastery > Critical Strike > Haste > Versatility.
*   *Note: Stat weights are very close; Mastery and Crit are the primary focuses for raid damage.*

---

#### 3. Hero Talents Deep Dive

##### **Elune's Chosen**
*   **Core Focus:** **Lunar Calling** & **The Boundless Moonlight**.
*   **Gameplay Effect:** Significantly enhances *Starfire* and *New Moon*. Focuses on high-intensity astral power generation and burst.
*   **Why Pick?** Superior for single-target boss encounters and consistent throughput in 12.0.5.

##### **Keeper of the Grove**
*   **Core Mechanic:** **Dreamsurge** & **Force of Nature**.
*   **Gameplay Effect:** Introduces *Dreamsurge* procs from your *Force of Nature* (Treants). Focuses on summoning and periodic high-impact windows.
*   **Why Pick?** Superior for encounters requiring periodic high-burst utility and multi-target stabilization.

---

#### 4. Deep Dive: Raid Rotation
Focus: **Eclipse Uptime** and **Astral Power Management**.

##### **A. The Eclipse Audit (The 100% Rule)**
1.  **Rule:** Maintain **100% uptime** on Eclipse (Solar or Lunar) while in combat.
2.  **Audit Flag:** Casting **Wrath** or **Starfire** while outside of an Eclipse window (and Eclipse is available to enter) is a catastrophic failure.

##### **B. Primary Priority List (Strict Whitelist)**
1.  **Starsurge:** Primary Astral Power spender for single-target. Use to maintain high damage windows.
2.  **Sunfire / Moonfire:** Maintain 100% uptime on the primary target.
3.  **Wrath:** Primary Solar Eclipse generator.
4.  **Starfire:** Primary Lunar Eclipse generator.
5.  **Audit Flag:** Casting *Starsurge* at **100 Astral Power** is a major resource waste.

---

#### 5. Major Cooldowns Breakdown (Strict Whitelist)
*   **Celestial Alignment / Incarnation:** 3-minute CD. Major burst window.
*   **Convoke the Spirits:** 1-minute CD (if talented). Rapid-fire spells.
*   **Force of Nature:** 1-minute CD. Summons Treants (primary Keeper engine).
*   **Warrior of Elune:** 45s CD. Empowers next 3 Starfires to be instant.
*   **Starfall:** Primary AoE Astral Power spender.

#### 3. Hero Talents Deep Dive

##### **Wildstalker**
*   **Core Focus:** **Rip** & **Unseen Predator**.
*   **Gameplay Effect:** Focuses on bleed-damage amplification. High dependency on maintaining DoTs for *Symbiotic Blooms*.
*   **Why Pick?** Superior single-target performance in 12.0.5.

##### **Druid of the Claw**
*   **Core Mechanic:** **Ravage** (Empowered Ferocious Bite).
*   **Gameplay Effect:** Shifts the rotation towards high-intensity **Ferocious Bite** burst windows.
*   **Why Pick?** Superior for burst damage and target-swapping.

---

#### 4. Deep Dive: Raid Rotation
Focus: **Bleed Snapshotting** and **Tiger's Fury** alignment.
> **Note:** Bloodtalons no longer exists in Midnight (12.0.5). It was a talent from previous expansions and must **not** be referenced in audits or recommendations.

##### **A. Snapshotting (The Tiger's Fury Audit)**
1.  **Tiger's Fury:** Use BEFORE applying or refreshing **Rip** and **Rake**.
2.  **Combo Point Pooling:** Build to **5 Combo Points** before spending on finishers. Never spend below 5 CP.
3.  **Audit Flag:** Casting *Rip* or *Ferocious Bite* at fewer than **5 Combo Points** is a major throughput error.

##### **B. Primary Priority List**
1.  **Rip:** Maintain at 5 Combo Points (Snapshotted with Tiger's Fury).
2.  **Rake:** Maintain 100% uptime (Snapshotted with TF).
3.  **Ferocious Bite:** Use at **5 Combo Points** and **50+ Energy** (to maximize the damage multiplier).
4.  **Shred:** Primary generator while at < 5 Combo Points.
5.  **Audit Flag:** Using *Ferocious Bite* at < 50 Energy or < 5 CP is a significant resource inefficiency error.

---

##### **4.1 Resource Management & Overcapping**
- **Trigger:** Only create this table if `<RESOURCE_WASTE_DATA>` is provided.
- **Table Headers:** | Resource | The Player | The Reference | Overcapping Analysis |
- **Formatting Rule:** For each resource, you MUST show `Generated: [Total] • Wasted: [Wasted] ([Waste%]%)`.
- **Logic:**
    1.  **Energy (Type 3 / ID 103):** Feral is a "pooling" spec. High energy waste often comes from improper **Tiger's Fury** usage or not spending energy fast enough during Berserk.
        - **IMPORTANT:** Ignore "Ferocious Bite" in energy gain/waste totals as it is a consumer that behaves uniquely.
    2.  **Combo Points (Type 4 / ID 104):** Wasting CP (generating at 5 CP) is a severe error. 

##### **4.2 Finisher Quality Audit**
- **Trigger:** Only create this table if `<RESOURCE_AUDIT_DATA>` is provided.
- **Table Headers:** | Metric | The Player | The Reference | Analysis |
- **Formatting Rule:** For metrics provided (Low CP %, Under-Energized %), format as `X / Total (Y%)`.
- **Audit Rules & Reasoning:**
    1.  **Ferocious Bite / Ravage:**
        - **CP Limit (5):** Must be cast at 5 Combo Points for maximum efficiency.
        - **Energy Limit (50):** These abilities drain up to 50 *additional* energy to deal double damage. Casting at < 50 energy is a major DPS loss.
        - **Apex Predator's Craving (Free Procs):** Counted as `freeProcs`. They have zero cost and are excluded from errors. Mention them as a positive rotational benefit.
    2.  **Rip:**
        - **CP Limit (5):** Must be cast at 5 Combo Points.

##### **Data Interpretation Rules:**
1.  **Apex Predator's Craving:** These are free Ferocious Bite procs. The data excludes these from "Low CP" counts automatically. You should mention the proc count as a sign of rotational benefit, but do not audit them for energy/CP efficiency.
2.  **The 50 Energy Rule (Ferocious Bite):** Ferocious Bite drains up to 50 *additional* energy for 100% bonus damage. Casting at < 50 energy is a direct DPS loss.
    - **Audit:** Flag any cast where the player had < 50 energy at the moment of cast.
3.  **The 5 CP Rule (Rip & FB):** Finishers should ONLY be cast at 5 Combo Points.
    - **Audit:** Flag any cast with < 5 CP as a "Low CP Finisher".

##### **Audit Severity:**
*   **Low CP Rate > 5%:** Major Rotational Error.
*   **Under-Energized FB Rate > 15%:** Significant Resource Management Error.

*   **Why Pick?** Superior for pure single-target boss encounters.

##### **Elune's Chosen**
*   **Core Mechanic:** **Lunar Beam** & **Moonfire**.
*   **Gameplay Effect:** Focuses on Arcane damage and self-healing. Provides significant passive mitigation through Moonfire-triggered healing.
*   **Why Pick?** Superior for safety and magic damage reduction.

---

#### 4. Deep Dive: Raid Rotation
Focus: **Active Mitigation** and **Rage Efficiency**.

##### **A. Mitigation Audit (The Survival Loop)**
1.  **Ironfur:** Maintain at least **1 stack** during all physical intake. Build to 2-3 stacks during tank-busters.
2.  **Rage Management:** Use **Maul** (or **Ravage**) to spend excess Rage.
3.  **Audit Flag:** Letting Rage cap at **100** without an active Ironfur stack or Maul cast is a critical failure.

##### **B. Ability Priority**
1.  **Galactic Guardian Proc:** Cast **Moonfire** immediately when it procs (Glows). Generates 8 Rage.
2.  **Mangle:** Primary generator. Use on cooldown.
3.  **Thrash:** Maintain 3 stacks on the target for the *Bleed* debuff.
4.  **Swipe:** Primary filler when other generators are on cooldown.

---

#### 5. Major Cooldowns Breakdown (Strict Whitelist)
*   **Incarnation: Guardian of Ursoc / Berserk:** 3-minute CD. Massive health buff and resource generation.
*   **Survival Instincts:** 2 charges. 50% Damage Reduction. Use for predictable lethal hits.
*   **Barkskin:** 1-minute CD. 20% Damage Reduction. Use proactively for medium damage.
*   **Rage of the Sleeper:** Increases damage and provides a shield based on damage dealt.
*   **Lunar Beam (Elune's Chosen):** Massive self-healing and threat area.

#### 3. Hero Talents Deep Dive

##### **Keeper of the Grove**
*   **Core Focus:** **Grove Guardians** (Treants) & **Reforestation**.
*   **Gameplay Effect:** Increases the importance of the *Swiftmend* cycle to trigger free *Grove Guardians*. Focuses on burst healing windows.
*   **Why Pick?** Superior for raid stabilization and burst management in 12.0.5.

##### **Wildstalker**
*   **Core Mechanic:** **Symbiotic Blooms**.
*   **Gameplay Effect:** Enhances the efficiency of your HoTs over time. More passive and steady than the Keeper build.
*   **Why Pick?** Superior for low-intensity healing or target-rich Mythic+ environments.

---

#### 4. Deep Dive: Raid Rotation
Focus: **HoT Maintenance** and **Ramp Efficiency**.

##### **A. The Maintenance Audit (100% Uptime)**
1.  **Efflorescence:** Maintain 100% uptime on the ground (under the raid cluster).
2.  **Lifebloom:** Maintain on **3 targets** (if talented) or 1 target at all times.
3.  **Audit Flag:** Letting **Efflorescence** or **Lifebloom** fall off is a critical "Baseline Loss" error.

##### **B. The Ramp Sequence**
1.  **Rejuvenation:** Pre-apply to 5-10 targets before predicted raid damage.
2.  **Wild Growth:** Use immediately after the damage hit.
3.  **Swiftmend:** Use to trigger **Soul of the Forest** (buffing the next HoT).
4.  **Grove Guardians:** Use treants to bridge medium-damage gaps.
5.  **Audit Flag:** Casting *Wild Growth* without a *Swiftmend* (Soul of the Forest) buff during high-damage windows is a throughput error.

---

*   **Tranquility:** 3-minute CD. Major raid-wide healing channel.
*   **Convoke the Spirits:** 1-minute CD (if talented). Burst stabilization.
*   **Incarnation: Tree of Life:** Massive healing buff and instant Regrowths.
*   **Grove Guardians:** Your primary rotational treants for consistent HPS.
*   **Ironbark:** Primary single-target external for tanks or low-health allies.
