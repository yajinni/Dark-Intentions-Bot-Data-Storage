### Balance Druid Knowledge Base (Patch 12.1) - RAID/M+ FOCUS

#### Current 12.1 Source Snapshot
Source verification date: **2026-09-17**. Current guide pages: [guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/balance-druid-pve-dps-guide) · [talents](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/balance-druid-pve-dps-spec-builds-talents) · [stats](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/balance-druid-pve-dps-stat-priority) · [enchants](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/balance-druid-pve-dps-gems-enchants-consumables) · [rotation](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/balance-druid-pve-dps-rotation-cooldowns-abilities).

The Icy Veins 12.1 recommendations below supersede the former gearing and rotation summary. Character-specific simulations and item level take priority whenever the guide calls out close or fluid stats.

**Talent and Hero Tree recommendation**
- Elune's Chosen is the default all-round raid/M+ tree; Keeper of the Grove is a burst-AoE/low-key option. The Apex talent is Ascendant Eclipses.

**Stat priority**
- Elune's Chosen: Intellect > Mastery > Haste > Critical Strike > Versatility. Keeper: Intellect > Mastery > Critical Strike > Haste > Versatility. Sim gear because the order is fluid.

**Gems**
- Indecipherable Eversong Diamond, then Masterful Lapis/Peridot/Garnet or Quick Amethyst according to the current weakest stat.

**Enchants and weapon augment**
- Thalassian Phoenix Oil; Arcane Mastery weapon enchant; Empowered Rune of Avoidance or Empowered Hex of Leeching; Amirdrassil's Grace or Silvermoon's Mending; Mark of the Worldsoul; Sunfire Silk Spellthread; Lynx's Dexterity or Shaladrassil's Roots; Eyes of the Eagle.

**Consumables**
- Use the flask that corrects the current secondary profile, typically Blood Knights or Magisters. Potion of Recklessness, current feast/personal food, Concentrated Silvermoon Health Potion and Void-Touched Augment Rune.

**Rotation and audit focus**
- Maintain Eclipse and the required DoTs, use Starweaver procs without wasting Starsurge/Starfall resources, and align Celestial Alignment, Force of Nature, Fury of Elune and Convoke with the chosen Hero Tree window.

**Talent and Hero Tree recommendation**
- Wildstalker and Druid of the Claw are very close in raid single target; Wildstalker transitions more easily to cleave, while Druid of the Claw edges Mythic+ through defense and lower execution demand.

**Stat priority**
- Prioritize Agility and item level, then use sims to balance the close secondary stats for the chosen Hero Tree and target profile.

**Gems**
- Indecipherable Eversong Diamond, then Flawless Quick Amethyst; use Powerful Eversong Diamond plus color activation when socket count supports it.

**Enchants and weapon augment**
- Rite of Hash'ey and Thalassian Phoenix Oil; Empowered Rune of Avoidance; Amirdrassil's Grace; Mark of the Worldsoul; Forest Hunter's Armor Kit; Lynx's Dexterity; Eyes of the Eagle.

**Consumables**
- Magisters for Claw or Blood Knights for Wildstalker when that best fits the stat profile; Potion of Recklessness; current feast/personal food and Void-Touched Augment Rune.

**Rotation and audit focus**
- Snapshot Tiger's Fury with bleeds and buffs, keep Rip/Rake uptime, spend resources without clipping strong bleeds, and align Berserk, Convoke and Ashamane's Guidance with the selected tree.

**Talent and Hero Tree recommendation**
- Druid of the Claw is strong for single-target and Incarnation pulls; Elune's Chosen excels in multi-target through Lunar Beam and self-healing. Both remain viable.

**Stat priority**
- Survivability: item level > Haste > Versatility > Mastery > Critical Strike. Damage: Haste > Versatility > Critical Strike > Mastery. Higher item level usually wins.

**Gems**
- Indecipherable Eversong Diamond, then Flawless Versatile Peridot with Haste/Versatility balance.

**Enchants and weapon augment**
- Berserker's Rage weapon enchant and Thalassian Phoenix Oil; Akil'zon's Swiftness; Empowered Blessing of Speed; Mark of the Worldsoul or Rootwarden's Binding; Forest Hunter's or Blood Knight's Armor Kit; Farstrider's Hunt; Silvermoon's Alacrity.

**Consumables**
- Blood Knights flask, Light's Potential, Harandar/Silvermoon feast or Royal Roast, health potion/Healthstone and Void-Touched Augment Rune.

**Rotation and audit focus**
- Generate and spend Rage on Ironfur while keeping the defensive buff active, maintain Moonfire/Thrash and Tooth and Claw effects, and plan Incarnation, Barkskin and Survival Instincts around the damage profile.

The Icy Veins 12.1 recommendations below supersede the former gearing and rotation summary. Character-specific simulations and item level take priority whenever the guide calls out close or fluid stats.

**Talent and Hero Tree recommendation**
- Wildstalker is the default for Mythic+ and small groups and is competitive in raid; Keeper of the Grove offers a burst-healing alternative.

**Stat priority**
- Raid: Intellect > Haste > Mastery > Versatility > Critical Strike. Dungeon gear is more profile-dependent; item level and encounter sims decide close upgrades.

**Gems**
- Indecipherable Eversong Diamond, then Flawless Masterful Peridot for Haste/Mastery.

**Enchants and weapon augment**
- Thalassian Phoenix Oil; Empowered Hex of Leeching; Silvermoon's Mending; Mark of the Worldsoul; Arcanoweave Spellthread; Shaladrassil's Roots; +29 Mastery rings; Acuity of the Ren'dorei weapon enchant.

**Consumables**
- Blood Knights flask for raid, Magisters for Mythic+ when throughput stats favor it; Potion of Recklessness rather than mana potions by default; Concentrated Silvermoon Health Potion; Silvermoon/Quel'Dorei/Harandar feast; Void-Touched Augment Rune.

**Rotation and audit focus**
- Maintain Lifebloom and Photosynthesis coverage, Rejuvenation for Abundance, Efflorescence and Swiftmend on cooldown, then Wild Growth for real group damage. Extend HoTs with Tranquility/Convoke and reserve Ironbark/Innervate for planned events.
