### 1. Audit Conclusions
   - **Performance Overview:**
       - Tattoomoon demonstrates a strong grasp of the Archon Holy Priest fundamental engine, maintaining solid uptimes on core buffs like [Divine Image](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=405963). However, the throughput gap (approx. 42K HPS) is primarily driven by lower efficiency in Holy Word usage and a critical lack of activity during low-intensity phases. The player completely neglected [Smite](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=585) usage, missing vital [Serendipity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=236299) CDR for Holy Words and [Surge of Light](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=114255) procs. Furthermore, a massive discrepancy in [Visage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=372014) uptime suggests positioning or form-management issues that impacted passive throughput.
   - **Casting Optimizations:**
       - **Prayer of Healing:** The player showed a -17.5% difference in casts. **Advice:** As an Archon Priest, [Prayer of Healing](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=596) is your primary consumer for [Surge of Light](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=114255) procs. Ensure every proc is spent here rather than [Flash Heal](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2061) to maximize mana efficiency and group throughput.
       - **Holy Word: Serenity:** -12.5% casts and -20.5% value. **Per Cast Reasoning:** The reference player has significantly higher Crit (28.6% vs 22.8%) and Mastery (20.1% vs 15.4%), which scales the "Per Cast" hit of Serenity much harder. **Advice:** Use [Holy Word: Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2050) more aggressively for triage. To bridge the value gap, ensure you are casting [Smite](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=585) to reset your Holy Words faster via [Serendipity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=236299).
       - **Flash Heal:** -50.0% casts and -35.3% value. **Per Cast Reasoning:** Reference value per cast was 57.8K vs Tattoomoon's 37.4K. This is due to the reference's superior stat scaling and better alignment with [Divine Image](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=392990) procs. **Advice:** While Archon prioritizes [Prayer of Healing](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=596) for procs, manual [Flash Heal](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2061) casts are still necessary to trigger [Lightweaver](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=391142) if talented, or to provide targeted triage when Holy Words are on cooldown.
       - **Holy Nova:** -28.6% casts. **Advice:** Use [Holy Nova](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=281265) during high-movement phases where hard-casting is impossible to maintain some level of [Echo of Light](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=77489) contribution.
       - **Benediction:** +22.2% casts. **Advice:** While the player had more applications, the overall impact was diluted by lower crit/mastery stats. Keep using [Prayer of Mending](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=33110) on cooldown to facilitate these procs.

### 4. Resource Management
The Player effectively managed mana but failed to maximize **Serendipity** and **Surge of Light** procs. Tattoomoon cast **0 Smites**, whereas the Reference utilized downtime to cast filler.

- **Surge of Light:** Archon Priests must spend procs on [Prayer of Healing](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=596). The player's lower cast count for PoH suggests either over-capping procs or ignoring them during high-intensity phases.
