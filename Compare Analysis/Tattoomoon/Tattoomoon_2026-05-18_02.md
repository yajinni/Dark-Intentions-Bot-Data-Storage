### 1. Audit Conclusions
   - **Performance Overview:**
       - Tattoomoon delivered a respectable performance at the 75th percentile, demonstrating a solid grasp of the Archon Holy Priest toolkit. However, a ~42K HPS gap exists compared to the reference. This delta is primarily driven by lower cast frequency of core Holy Words and efficient fillers, alongside significantly lower secondary stat scaling (Crit/Mastery) which reduced the raw throughput of every individual cast.
       - Resource management was generally correct for the Archon spec, avoiding the common pitfall of wasting [Surge of Light](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=114255) procs on [Flash Heal](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2061), though lower overall activity (APM) resulted in fewer [Holy Word: Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2050) resets through Serendipity.
   - **Casting Optimizations:**
       - **[Prayer of Healing](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=596):** Casts were 17.5% lower than the reference. **Advice:** In the Archon build, ensure you are utilizing [Spiritwell](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=449840) procs to fuel [Prayer of Healing](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=596) during high-intensity raid damage.
       - **[Holy Word: Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2050):** Showed a 12.5% deficit in casts and a 20.5% deficit in per-cast healing. **Per Cast Reasoning:** The player's Crit (22.78%) and Mastery (15.39%) are significantly lower than the reference's 28.65% and 20.09%, leading to weaker individual heals. **Advice:** Use [Smite](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=585) during downtime to accelerate cooldown reduction via Serendipity, ensuring more Holy Words are available for burst windows.
       - **[Holy Nova](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=281265):** Cast count was 28.6% lower. **Advice:** While situational, ensure you are using [Holy Nova](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=281265) as a low-cost filler or during movement phases when higher priority spells are on cooldown.
       - **[Flash Heal](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2061):** Casts were 50% lower, with a 35.3% lower per-cast value. **Per Cast Reasoning:** Driven by lower Intellect and secondary stat scaling compared to the reference. **Advice:** While [Flash Heal](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2061) is de-emphasized in Archon, maintain enough usage to keep [Protective Light](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=193065) active for personal damage reduction.
       - **[Benediction](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1262763):** Casts were 22.2% higher. **Analysis:** This is a positive indicator of proactive application, though it could not offset the losses in larger throughput spells.
       - **[Desperate Prayer](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=19236):** 100% difference (Player used it, Reference did not). **Analysis:** Good use of personal defensives to survive high-damage mechanics.

### 4. Resource Management (Energy/Combo Points/Mana)
As an Archon Holy Priest, the priority is to maintain Holy Word uptime and leverage [Spiritwell](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=449840). The player correctly used [Halo](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=120517) on cooldown (5 casts) to trigger Archon buffs. However, the lower count of [Holy Word: Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2050) suggests missed opportunities for Serendipity reduction. The Spec Knowledge Base notes that [Surge of Light](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=114255) procs should be spent on [Prayer of Healing](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=596) in this build; while Tattoomoon avoided "wasting" them on [Flash Heal](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2061), the overall cast volume was too low to maximize the [Spiritwell](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=449840) synergy.
