### 1. Audit Conclusions
   - **Performance Overview:**
       - Tattoomoon demonstrated a solid grasp of the Archon Holy Priest toolkit, achieving a respectable 75% parse. The primary bottleneck preventing a higher parse is a combination of significantly lower secondary stat optimization (Crit and Mastery) compared to the reference and lower overall casting frequency (CPM). 
       - While the player correctly utilized the [Spiritwell](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=453846) synergy by consuming [Surge of Light](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=114255) procs on [Prayer of Healing](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=596), the raw throughput per cast was stifled by gear and stat choices. Activity levels were also lower than the reference, leading to missed opportunities for [Serendipity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=373456) resets.
   - **Casting Optimizations:**
       - **[Prayer of Healing](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=596):** Casts were **17.5% lower** than the reference. In an Archon build, this is your primary consumer for [Surge of Light](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=114255) procs. **Advice:** Ensure you are prioritizing these procs immediately to maximize the [Spiritwell](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=453846) throughput.
       - **[Holy Word: Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2050):** Casts were **12.5% lower** and per-cast value was **20.5% lower**. **Advice:** The value difference is a technical result of your lower Crit (22.78% vs 28.65%) and Mastery (15.39% vs 20.09%). To fix the cast count, ensure you are using [Smite](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=585) or [Flash Heal](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2061) during low-damage windows to reset Holy Words faster.
       - **[Echo of Light](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=77489):** Per-tick value was **26.5% lower** than the reference. **Advice:** This is a direct consequence of your Mastery rating being 216 points lower and your Crit chance being nearly 6% lower. Mastery is the primary engine for [Echo of Light](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=77489) scaling.
       - **[Flash Heal](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2061):** Casts were **50% lower**. **Advice:** While Archon de-emphasizes [Flash Heal](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2061) in favor of [Prayer of Healing](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=596), you still need to weave it to maintain [Lightweaver](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=390911) if talented, or to provide triage when [Surge of Light](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=114255) isn't active.
       - **[Holy Nova](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=281265):** Casts were **28.6% lower**. **Advice:** Use [Holy Nova](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=281265) more aggressively during high-movement phases where hard-casting is impossible to maintain some level of [Echo of Light](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=77489) rolling on the raid.

This ensures maximum [Spiritwell](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=453846) value. However, the player lacked any [Smite](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=585) usage during the encounter. In Holy Priest gameplay, [Smite](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=585) is essential during low-healing demand to trigger [Serendipity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=373456) for Holy Word resets and to fish for [Surge of Light](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=114255) procs.
