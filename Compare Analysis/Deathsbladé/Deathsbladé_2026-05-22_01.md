### Encounter: Imperator Averzian (Mythic)
### 1. Audit Conclusions
- **Performance Overview:**
    - Deathsbladé maintained strong core survivability uptimes (98.4% Bone Shield and 100% Sanguine Ground), but overall DPS throughput was significantly hampered by rotational gaps and suboptimal cooldown management. The player’s lower Activity/APM (240 vs 287 casts) despite a longer fight duration suggests significant downtime. Resource management was generally sound regarding runes, though Runic Power waste (9.8%) indicates missed [Death Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49998) opportunities or over-capping during high-generation windows.
- **Casting Optimizations:**
    - [Death Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49998): (-24.2% Casts, -17.8% Per Cast). The player cast this significantly less than the reference. The lower value per cast is attributed to a -26.1% Strength deficit and lower Mastery. **Advice:** Ensure Runic Power is pooled to use [Death Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49998) reactively after large hits or proactively to prevent capping above 80 RP.
    - [Exterminate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=441424): (+15.0% Casts, -28.1% Per Cast). Despite casting more frequently, the damage per cast was drastically lower. This suggests the player is not aligning [Exterminate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=441424) with peak damage windows or [Dancing Rune Weapon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1264353). **Advice:** [Exterminate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=441424) should be consumed immediately after [Reaper's Mark](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=439843) expires; ensure you are standing within [Sanguine Ground](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=391458) for the 5% damage bonus.
    - [Reaper's Mark](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=439843): (-22.2% Casts, -23.8% Per Cast). The player missed at least two possible casts of their primary Hero Spec mechanic. **Advice:** Use [Reaper's Mark](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=439843) strictly on cooldown as it drives your entire burst profile and [Exterminate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=441424) procs.
    - [Blood Boil](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=50842): (-19.6% Casts, -48.9% Per Cast). The massive value gap is primarily due to target density; the reference hit 125 targets vs the player's 64. **Advice:** In Mythic Imperator Averzian, utilize [Blood Boil](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=50842) aggressively during add spawns to maximize cleave and maintain [Blood Plague](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=55078).
    - [Heart Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=206930): (-29.0% Casts, -33.7% Per Cast). Significant downtime in filler usage. **Advice:** Use [Heart Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=206930) as your primary Runic Power generator when [Marrowrend](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=195182) is not required for Bone Shield stacks.
    - [Death and Decay](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=43265): (+50.0% Casts, -48.1% Per Cast). The player casted this more often but gained less value. **Advice:** Ensure [Death and Decay](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=43265) is placed when enemies are grouped to maximize [Sanguine Ground](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=391458) benefit and cleave damage.
    - [Dancing Rune Weapon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1264353): (-64.0% Per Cast). Technical reason: The reference aligned [Dancing Rune Weapon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1264353) with significantly higher target density and achieved 30% more uptime on [Dance of Midnight](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1264407). **Advice:** Coordinate [Dancing Rune Weapon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1264353) with high-density add phases to maximize the mirrored attack value.
