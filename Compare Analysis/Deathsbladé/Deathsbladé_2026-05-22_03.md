    - [Reaper's Mark](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=439843): 22.2% fewer casts. **Advice:** This is your highest priority Deathbringer ability. Use it strictly on cooldown to maximize its explosion damage and trigger [Exterminate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=441424).

    - [Blood Boil](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=50842): 19.6% fewer casts and 48.9% lower value. **Advice:** Use Blood Boil to maintain [Blood Plague](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=55078) and as a filler during cleave situations when Death and Decay is active.
    - [Heart Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=206930): 29.0% fewer casts. **Advice:** Use this as your primary Runic Power generator. You left significant RP on the table by under-utilizing this filler.

    - [Marrowrend](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=195182): 20% more casts. **Advice:** This indicates over-investment in Bone Shield stacks. Only cast Marrowrend when stacks are below 7.
    - [Raise Dead](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=46585): 33.3% fewer casts. **Advice:** Ensure this is active for every pull; the reference used it more frequently due to death/respawn timings or specific window alignment.
    - [Death's Caress](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=195292): 33.3% fewer casts. **Advice:** Only use this for pulling or maintaining Bone Shield when out of melee range.
