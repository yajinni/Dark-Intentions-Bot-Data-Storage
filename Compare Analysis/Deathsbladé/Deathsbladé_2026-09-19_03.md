### 1. Audit Conclusions
- **Performance Overview:**
    - The player's performance significantly trails the reference, primarily due to low rotational activity and weak cooldown alignment. While the player maintained core defensive buffs, the total throughput was severely impacted by infrequent usage of key offensive abilities and underutilization of the [Dancing Rune Weapon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49028) window.
    - Resource management was suboptimal; while not heavily overcapping Runic Power, the player frequently allowed resources to sit idle, missing out on crucial [Heart Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=206930) and [Blood Boil](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=50842) casts.
- **Casting Optimizations:**
    - [Blood Boil](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=50842): Player 11 casts (2.33 CPM), Reference 41 casts (8.72 CPM). Total damage -82.7%.
        - **Advice:** The massive delta in [Blood Boil](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=50842) usage is the primary driver of the DPS gap. Ensure you are using this ability to spread [Blood Plague](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=55078) and maintain threat; the reference's significantly higher cast count suggests a more aggressive approach to multi-target damage and resource spending.
    - [Dancing Rune Weapon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49028): Player 4 casts (0.85 CPM), Reference 4 casts (0.85 CPM). Total damage -60.5%.
        - **Advice:** While cast counts are identical, the player's damage per cast is 60.2% lower. This indicates poor alignment with external raid buffs or missing offensive procs/trinket windows. Work on syncing this major cooldown with peak burst windows or high-threat phases.
    - [Heart Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=206930): Player 58 casts (12.26 CPM), Reference 80 casts (17.01 CPM). Total damage -43.4%.
        - **Advice:** Low cast counts indicate inefficient rune spending. Prioritize [Heart Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=206930) as your primary filler to maximize Runic Power generation and rune turnover.
    - [Death Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49998): Player 49 casts (10.36 CPM), Reference 67 casts (14.25 CPM). Total damage -43.0%.
        - **Advice:** Reduced usage suggests either high health maintenance (less need for active mitigation) or poor resource management. Balance [Death Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49998) usage to ensure you aren't starving your rotation of Runic Power while staying alive.

- **Cooldown Window Conclusions:**
    - The player's [Dancing Rune Weapon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49028) performance was substantially weaker than the reference, with a 44% loss in total damage during active windows. The player utilized 12 windows compared to the reference's 15, and the average damage per window was 29.6% lower. This confirms a lack of aggressive execution during high-impact moments.
