### Encounter: Imperator Averzian (Mythic)
### 1. Audit Conclusions
- **Performance Overview:**
    Deathsbladé’s performance was significantly hindered by a critical mismatch in target priority and cooldown alignment. While the player maintained a 98% focus on the primary boss, the reference log shows a highly efficient cleave strategy, dealing nearly 50% of their damage to high-priority adds like Abyssal Voidshapers and Malus. This lack of target density resulted in much lower "Per Cast" values for core cleave abilities. Additionally, Deathsbladé exhibited lower overall activity (APM) and sub-optimal Runic Power spending, leading to lower [Death Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49998) counts. Resource management was generally stable, though one [Bone Shield](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=195181) drop was recorded, which is a significant survival risk.
- **Casting Optimizations:**
    - [Dancing Rune Weapon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49028): **Value Delta: -64.0%**. While the player casted this more frequently (4 vs. 3), the reference achieved nearly triple the damage. **Advice:** Ensure DRW is synced with high-density add spawns (Voidshaper/Malus) to maximize the mirrored Heart Strikes and Blood Boils. Standing in [Sanguine Ground](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=391458) is mandatory.
    - [Death Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49998): **Casts Delta: -24.2%**. The player is under-utilizing their primary spender. **Advice:** Do not sit on Runic Power. Proactively use Death Strike to avoid capping (> 75 RP) even if health is high to maintain throughput and [Blood Shield](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=77535) uptime.
    - [Heart Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=206930): **Casts Delta: -29.0%**. Significant gap in filler usage. **Advice:** Heart Strike is your primary RP generator; ensure you are not letting Runes sit idle.
    - [Blood Boil](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=50842): **Value Delta: -48.9%**. Massive difference in damage per hit. **Technical Reason:** This is due to target density; the reference hit 125 targets across 51 casts, whereas the player only hit 64 targets across 41 casts. **Advice:** Prioritize Blood Boil usage when multiple adds are within range.
    - [Exterminate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=441424): **Value Delta: -28.1%**. Per cast damage is significantly lower. **Technical Reason:** Likely caused by the player's significantly lower Strength (-673) and missing target count during the Exterminate window.
    - [Death and Decay](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=43265): **Value Delta: -48.1%**. The player cast this more often (+50.0%) but achieved half the damage. **Advice:** Focus on placing D&D where it will hit the most targets, specifically the adds that spawn throughout the Imperator encounter.
