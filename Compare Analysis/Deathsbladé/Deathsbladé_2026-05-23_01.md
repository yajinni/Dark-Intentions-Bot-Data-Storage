# Deathsbladé (38% DPS Parse) vs. 胤胤怪 (99% DPS Parse)
<small>*(Player: 47.6K DPS | Reference: 80.6K DPS)*</small>

### Encounter: Imperator Averzian (Mythic)
### 1. Audit Conclusions
- **Performance Overview:**
    Deathsbladé's performance is significantly hindered by poor target prioritization and lower overall activity compared to the reference. The 38% parse is primarily the result of "tunnel-visioning" the boss; while the player maintained 98% damage on Imperator Averzian, the reference utilized cleave opportunities on adds (Abyssal Voidshapers and Malus) to nearly double the total throughput. Resource management was generally stable, though inefficient [Runic Power](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=106) spending (204 wasted) and a critical [Bone Shield](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=195181) drop indicate gaps in defensive-rotational maintenance.
- **Casting Optimizations:**
    - [Death Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49998): **24.2% fewer casts**. The player is pooling Runic Power too aggressively or missing opportunities to convert resources into healing/damage. Aim to use [Death Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49998) to prevent capping Runic Power (> 80 RP) even if health is high.
    - [Exterminate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=441424): **28.1% lower damage per cast**. This gap is driven by a massive difference in **Primary Stat (Strength -26.1%)** and Mastery. To improve this, ensure [Exterminate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=441424) procs are used while standing in [Death and Decay](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=43265) for the [Sanguine Ground](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=391458) bonus.
    - [Reaper's Mark](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=439843): **22.2% fewer casts**. As a Deathbringer, this is your primary rotational priority. It must be sent on cooldown to maximize the number of [Exterminate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=441424) procs generated.
    - [Heart Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=206930): **29.0% fewer casts**. This suggests significant downtime or "dead GCDs." Use [Heart Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=206930) more aggressively as a filler to generate Runic Power when [Bone Shield](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=195181) is healthy.
    - [Blood Boil](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=50842): **48.9% lower damage per cast**. This technical difference is due to **target density**. The reference hits significantly more targets (Adds) per cast. Ensure you are utilizing [Blood Boil](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=50842) when adds are present to spread [Blood Plague](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=55078) and maximize cleave.
    - [Death and Decay](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=43265): **48.1% lower damage per cast**. Despite casting it 50% more often, the damage is much lower. This confirms the player is not positioning the zone to hit the adds that spawn during the encounter. Position [Death and Decay](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=43265) to cover both the boss and incoming priority adds.
