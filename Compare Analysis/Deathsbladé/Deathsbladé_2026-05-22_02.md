### 1. Audit Conclusions
- **Performance Overview:**
    - Deathsbladé's performance is characterized by a "boss-tunneling" approach, focusing 98% of damage on Imperator Averzian while the reference log actively cleaves and prioritizes high-value adds like Voidshapers and Malus. This lack of target switching is the primary driver of the parse difference.
    - Resource management shows significant room for improvement, particularly regarding [Marrowrend](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=195182) usage which resulted in 112 Runic Power wasted.
    - The player maintained high uptime on [Sanguine Ground](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=391458), but the overall throughput is hindered by lower primary stats (Strength difference of -673).
- **Casting Optimizations:**
    - [Death Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49998): -24.2% Casts | -17.8% Per Cast. **Advice:** The lower per-cast value is tied to significantly lower Strength (-26%). Ensure you are pooling Runic Power to [Death Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49998) reactively after large hits to maximize the heal and [Blood Shield](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=77535) size.
    - [Exterminate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=441424): +15.0% Casts | -28.1% Per Cast. **Advice:** The damage discrepancy suggests [Exterminate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=441424) procs are not being utilized within high-density windows. Ensure this is cast immediately after [Reaper's Mark](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=439843) expires on priority targets.
    - [Reaper's Mark](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=439843): -22.2% Casts. **Advice:** This is your primary [Deathbringer](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=439843) mechanic. You must use it on cooldown to trigger [Exterminate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=441424) and the associated damage reduction windows.
    - [Blood Boil](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=50842): -19.6% Casts | -48.9% Per Cast. **Advice:** The massive value gap is due to target density. The reference is using [Blood Boil](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=50842) to hit multiple adds (Voidshapers/Malus), whereas you are mostly hitting the boss. Improve target awareness to utilize cleave.
    - [Heart Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=206930): -29.0% Casts | -33.7% Per Cast. **Advice:** You are under-utilizing your primary Runic Power generator. Ensure you spend Runes on [Heart Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=206930) whenever [Bone Shield](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=195181) is healthy (>7 stacks) and [Death and Decay](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=43265) is active for the 3-target cleave.
    - [Death and Decay](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=43265): +50.0% Casts | -48.1% Per Cast. **Advice:** While you are casting it more, you are gaining less value. This indicates you are placing it where adds are not present or moving out of it too quickly. Align placement with add spawn locations.
