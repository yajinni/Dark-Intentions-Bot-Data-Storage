### Encounter: Vorasius (Normal)
### 1. Audit Conclusions
- **Performance Overview:**
    - Sylleena demonstrates a functional understanding of the Holy Priest kit but suffers from significant downtime and suboptimal spell priority for the **Oracle** hero spec. The primary driver for the parse difference is a failure to utilize the [Flash Heal](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2061) and [Heal](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2060) weaving required to generate [Surge of Light](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=114255) procs and maintain high [Divine Image](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=405963) uptime.
    - The player's HPS was further hindered by missing core rotational abilities like [Holy Fire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=14914) and [Smite](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=585), which provide essential cooldown reduction for Holy Words via Serendipity.
    - Resource management was inefficient; the lack of filler spells (Smite) during low-damage phases resulted in missed proc opportunities and slower [Holy Word: Chastise](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=88625) resets, which indirectly lowers the frequency of all Holy Words.
- **Casting Optimizations:**
    - [Flash Heal](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2061): **Advice:** Sylleena cast this only 5 times compared to the reference's 52. You are ignoring the [Lightweaver](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=391381) loop. Ensure you use [Flash Heal](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2061) to buff your [Heal](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2060) or [Prayer of Healing](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=596) and to spend [Surge of Light](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=114255) procs.
    - [Holy Word: Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2050): **Advice:** You cast this 15 times vs. 24. The 50% lower healing per cast is due to the reference utilizing [Desperate Times (Rank 2)](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=391381) and maintaining higher [Divine Image](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=405963) uptime. Prioritize casting Holy Words while [Divine Image](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=405963) is active.
    - [Holy Word: Sanctify](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=34861): **Advice:** Despite having more casts (13 vs 10), your per-cast effectiveness was 53% lower. This indicates poor target density or casting when the raid is at high health. Align [Holy Word: Sanctify](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=34861) with actual raid damage spikes.
    - [Prayer of Healing](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=596): **Advice:** You used this 24 times while the reference used it 0. In an Oracle build, [Prayer of Healing](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=596) is generally less efficient than weaving [Flash Heal](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2061) and [Heal](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2060). Shift these casts toward [Heal](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2060) or Smite filler.
    - [Benediction](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1262763): **Advice:** Your per-cast value was 71.8% lower. The reference benefits from the [Benediction 2 Holy (Rank 2)](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1262758) talent which significantly scales the Renew application. Review your talent choice here.
    - [Holy Nova](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=281265): **Advice:** You cast this 10 times vs. 0. [Holy Nova](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=281265) is extremely low throughput in a raid setting. Replace these GCDs with [Smite](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=585) or [Renew](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=139).
    - [Prayer of Mending](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=33110): **Advice:** While cast counts were identical, your per-cast value was 27% lower. This is due to missing [Holy Celerity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1215275) and [Prompt Prognosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=443314) (Oracle synergy) which the reference utilized.
