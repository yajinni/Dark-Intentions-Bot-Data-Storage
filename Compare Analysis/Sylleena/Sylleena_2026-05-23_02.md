# Sylleena (28% HPS Parse) vs. Krixxa (97% HPS Parse)
<small>*(Player: 84.3K HPS | Reference: 156.2K HPS)*</small>

### Encounter: Fallen-King Salhadaar (Heroic)
### 1. Audit Conclusions
- **Performance Overview:**
    - Sylleena’s performance is significantly hindered by a lack of active filler usage and poor cooldown alignment. While the core casting pattern exists, the frequency of high-impact spells like [Holy Word: Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2050) is too low, likely due to a total absence of filler activity to reset cooldowns.
    - The throughput gap is exacerbated by suboptimal [Archon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=449840) synergy, specifically regarding the utilization of [Surge of Light](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=114255) procs and [Halo](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=120517) timing.
    - Resource management is fundamentally flawed; by failing to cast [Smite](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=585), the player misses out on critical [Serendipity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=236299) resets and free procs, leading to long periods of downtime and lower overall Holy Word frequency (Section 5).
- **Casting Optimizations:**
    - **[Holy Word: Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2050):** Casts are 30.3% lower than the reference, and healing per cast is 45.2% lower. **Advice:** The lower per-cast value is driven by a significantly lower Critical Strike rating (18.9% vs 25.3%) and missing [Divine Image](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=392990) uptime. Ensure you are casting [Smite](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=585) during low damage phases to reset this spell's cooldown more frequently via Serendipity.
    - **[Prayer of Healing](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=596):** Per cast value is 28.7% lower. **Advice:** As an [Archon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=449840) Holy Priest, you must prioritize using [Surge of Light](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=114255) procs on [Prayer of Healing](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=596) due to the [Spiritwell](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=453112) synergy. This increases efficiency and raw throughput compared to using procs on [Flash Heal](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2061).
    - **[Halo](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=120517):** Per cast value is 55.7% lower despite casting it more frequently. **Advice:** [Halo](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=120517) is your primary trigger for [Archon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=449840) burst windows. The massive value difference suggests poor timing relative to raid damage or casting it when the raid is not positioned correctly to receive all pulses.
    - **[Flash Heal](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2061):** Casts are 42.9% lower and value per cast is 18.6% lower. **Advice:** Maintain better [Lightweaver](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=390993) weaving. The reference maintains a higher frequency of [Flash Heal](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2061) to empower subsequent [Heal](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2060) or [Prayer of Healing](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=596) casts.
    - **[Benediction](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1262763):** Total value is 59.5% lower. **Advice:** This is an automatic proc from [Renew](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=139) ticks. Your lower [Renew](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=139) uptime (77% vs 96%) directly translates to fewer [Benediction](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1262763) procs and lower passive healing.
    - **[Prayer of Mending](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=33110):** Casts are 10.0% lower. **Advice:** This is your primary engine. It should be cast strictly on cooldown. Missing even a few casts over a 4.5-minute fight results in lost [Echo of Light](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=77489) and proc opportunities.

### 5. Resource Management
Analysis of the provided logs reveals a complete failure to utilize the Holy Priest's primary filler and reset mechanic.

- **Filler Usage & Serendipity:** Sylleena cast **0** [Smite](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=585) over the entire encounter. According to the **Holy Priest Knowledge Base**, [Smite](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=585) is essential during low-intensity phases to fish for [Surge of Light](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=114255) procs and to trigger [Serendipity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=236299), which reduces the cooldown of Holy Words. 
- **Holy Word Uptime:** The reference's higher HPS is largely driven by having [Holy Word: Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2050) available 30% more often. By not casting [Smite](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=585), Sylleena is manually waiting for long cooldowns rather than actively reducing them, resulting in significantly lower throughput during peak damage windows.
- **Archon Synergy:** As an Archon, your [Surge of Light](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=114255) procs must be used on [Prayer of Healing](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=596). Failing to cast [Smite](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=585) removes a major source of these procs, further starving your mana efficiency and Archon burst potential.
