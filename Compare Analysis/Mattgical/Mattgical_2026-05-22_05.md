### 1. Audit Conclusions

    - Mattgical demonstrates a solid understanding of the Archon Hero Spec mechanics, particularly regarding [Halo](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=120644) usage and [Voidform](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=194249) uptime. However, the throughput gap (approx. 27K DPS) is primarily driven by lower [Haste](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1235108) and [Mastery](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=244031) ratings, alongside rotational inefficiencies in filler management.
    - Resource management was efficient with zero [Insanity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=391403) wasted, but overall generation was lower than the reference, indicating missed global cooldowns (GCDs).
- **Casting Optimizations:**
    - **[Mind Flay: Insanity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=391403):** Per Cast value is **27.6% lower** than the reference. This is largely due to the player's significantly lower Mastery (20.7% vs 23.6%) and Haste (20.5% vs 25.1%). Advice: Prioritize Mastery/Haste on gear as per the [Stat Priority](#2-stat-priority).
    - **[Vampiric Touch](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=34914):** Mattgical cast this 9 times compared to the reference's 1 cast, despite having the [Misery](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=238558) talent. Advice: Avoid manual refreshes of Vampiric Touch when [Misery](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=238558) is talented; rely on [Mind Blast](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=8092) to maintain DoT uptime.
    - **[Mind Flay](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=15407):** The player used this filler significantly more (+42.2% casts) but with **32.3% lower** damage per cast. Advice: Ensure channels are completed and only used when higher priority generators like [Mind Blast](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=8092) are on cooldown.
    - **[Halo](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=120644):** Per cast value is **35.0% higher** than the reference. This suggests excellent alignment with [Power Infusion](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=10060) or high crit RNG. Advice: Continue aligning Halo with major burst windows.
    - **[Psychic Link](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=199486):** Total damage is **12.3% lower** and per-hit value is **11.6% lower**. Technical Reason: Psychic Link scales directly with Mastery: Shadow Weaving; the player's lower Mastery rating and slightly lower DoT uptime on secondary targets like *Colossal Horror* reduced this throughput. Advice: Ensure [Vampiric Touch](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=34914) is active on all priority adds immediately.
    - **[Tentacle Slam](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1227621):** Per cast value is **19.3% lower**. Technical Reason: Lower overall stat scaling and potentially reduced uptime during [Voidform](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=194249) windows where [Idol of C'Thun](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=377349) procs are most valuable.
