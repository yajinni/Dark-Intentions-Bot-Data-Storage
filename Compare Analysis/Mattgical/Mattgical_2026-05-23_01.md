### Encounter: Chimaerus, the Undreamt God (Mythic)
### 1. Audit Conclusions
    - **Performance Overview:**
        - Mattgical delivered a respectable performance, landing in the 51st percentile. While the core Archon rotation is established, there are significant efficiency gaps compared to the 99th percentile reference. The player's throughput was hampered by suboptimal buff alignment and lower haste, leading to fewer overall casts in a longer fight.
        - Resource management was excellent, with 0% Insanity waste recorded, indicating that the player is spending resources promptly. However, the conversion of those resources into high-value damage windows (like Voidform) was less effective than the reference.
    - **Casting Optimizations:**
        - [Vampiric Touch](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=34914): Mattgical cast this 9 times (+800%) compared to the reference's 1 cast. The per-cast value was 89.1% lower. **Advice:** You are manually refreshing dots too frequently. Ensure you are utilizing [Misery](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=238558) and automated refresh mechanics to minimize GCDs spent on maintenance.
        - [Mind Flay](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=15407): Mattgical had 42.2% more casts but 32.3% lower per-cast value. **Advice:** This indicates you are using Mind Flay as a filler during low-value windows. Align your filler usage with [Sustained Potency](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=454002) and [Insidious Ire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=373213) to maximize tick damage.
        - [Mind Flay: Insanity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=391403): Per-cast value was 27.6% lower than the reference. **Technical Reason:** Reference had significantly higher Haste (25% vs 20%) and Mastery (23% vs 20%), which directly scales the tick rate and damage of this empowered filler. **Advice:** Prioritize Haste/Mastery gear and ensure this is cast strictly within high-uptime buff windows.
        - [Shadow Word: Pain](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=589): The reference manually cast this 40 times, while you relied entirely on passive application via VT/Misery. **Advice:** While Misery is standard, the reference's manual application on secondary targets (Horrors/Shades) significantly increased their Psychic Link and Shadowy Apparition generation. Use manual dots more aggressively on high-health secondary targets.
        - [Tentacle Slam](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1227621): Per-cast value was 19.3% lower. **Technical Reason:** Lower Mastery scaling (20% vs 23%) on this Idol of C'Thun proc. **Advice:** No direct rotational fix, but improving your Mastery stat will bridge this gap.
