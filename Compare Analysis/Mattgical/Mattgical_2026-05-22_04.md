### Encounter: Chimaerus, the Undreamt God (Mythic)
### 1. Audit Conclusions
- **Performance Overview:**
    - Mattgical delivered a mid-range performance characterized by solid resource management but significant rotational inefficiencies compared to the top-tier reference. While the player successfully avoided Insanity waste, their throughput was hampered by suboptimal spell selection (too much manual dotting) and lower stat scaling (significantly less Haste and Mastery). The gap in damage is primarily due to the reference's superior execution within [Voidform](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=194249) windows and better alignment of [Mind Flay: Insanity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=391403) with active buffs.
- **Casting Optimizations:**
    - [Psychic Link](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=199486): Total damage was 12.3% lower than the reference. This passive damage is tied to your primary target rotation; the reference's higher Haste allowed for more global cooldowns to funnel damage through this link.
    - [Mind Flay: Insanity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=391403): Total damage was 27.6% lower. Your per-cast average was 91.7K vs. the reference's 126.7K (a 27.6% difference). Technical Reason: This is largely due to lower [Mastery](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=77486) scaling and lower uptime on [Insidious Ire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=373213) (72.2% vs. 80.6%). Advice: Ensure you have maximal DoT uptime on the target before channeling to benefit from Mastery: Shadow Weaving.
    - [Vampiric Touch](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=34914): You cast this 9 times while the reference cast it only once. Total damage was 89.1% lower. Your per-cast value was significantly lower. Technical Reason: You are manually refreshing DoTs too often. Advice: With the [Misery](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=238558) talent, [Shadow Word: Pain](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=589) also applies [Vampiric Touch](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=34914). Trust your talent procs and cleave mechanics (like Shadow Crash) to maintain DoTs rather than manual hard-casting.
    - [Halo](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=120644): You had 16.7% more casts and 35.0% higher total damage. Your per-cast was 310.2K vs. 229.8K. Advice: You used [Halo](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=120644) very effectively during high target density; continue prioritizing this as an Archon.
    - [Mind Flay](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=15407): You cast this 42.2% more often but saw 32.3% lower value per cast (30.5K vs 45.1K). Technical Reason: Lower stat scaling and channeling into targets without full DoT priority. Advice: Avoid using basic Mind Flay as a filler if [Mind Blast](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=8092) or other high-priority generators are available.
    - [Tentacle Slam](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1227621): Total damage was 19.3% lower despite similar cast counts. Technical Reason: This scales with your primary stats and Mastery; the reference's gear provided higher baseline power for this proc.
