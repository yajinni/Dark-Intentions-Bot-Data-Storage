### Encounter: Chimaerus, the Undreamt God (Mythic)

### 1. Audit Conclusions
- **Performance Overview:**
    Mattgical's performance is characterized by a fundamental rotational error involving the over-prioritization of low-impact filler. While the player maintains decent uptime on major cooldowns, their core priority list is severely compromised by excessive manual casting of [Shadow Word: Pain](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=589) (876 casts), which is unnecessary given the [Misery](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=238558) talent. This "filler-spam" behavior significantly pushed out higher-value casts like [Mind Blast](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=8092) and [Shadow Word: Madness](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=335467), leading to the 34K DPS deficit.
- **Casting Optimizations:**
    - [Shadow Word: Pain](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=589): **(+2333.3% Casts)** The player casted this 876 times compared to the reference's 36. Since you have the [Misery](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=238558) talent, [Vampiric Touch](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=34914) automatically applies this dot. **Advice:** Stop manually casting this spell; it is a massive waste of GCDs that should be spent on [Mind Blast](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=8092) or Insanity spenders.
    - [Shadow Word: Madness](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=335467): **(-18.0% Casts)** You missed approximately 11 casts of this high-priority Archon ability. **Advice:** Ensure this is cast immediately upon procs to maximize Archon throughput.
    - [Mind Blast](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=8092): **(-12.3% Casts | -19.3% Per Cast)** You missed 8 casts of your primary generator. The lower per-cast value is due to the reference's higher crit rate (56.9% vs 50.9%) and better [Screams of the Void](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=393919) uptime. **Advice:** Use [Mind Blast](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=8092) on cooldown to avoid capping charges.
    - [Mind Flay: Insanity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=391403): **(-39.4% Per Cast)** Your hits were significantly weaker. Technical Reason: The reference had a 32.6% crit rate on this spell vs. your 18.8%, and better alignment with [Sustained Potency](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=454002). **Advice:** Only consume these procs when you have high-value buffs active or need to move.
    - [Vampiric Touch](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=34914): **(+200.0% Casts | -70.5% Per Cast)** You cast this 9 times vs. 3. **Advice:** Trust your DoT durations; excessive refreshing lowers your overall GCD efficiency.
    - [Void Volley](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1242189): **(-23.1% Casts)** You missed several opportunities to trigger this during [Voidform](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=194249). **Advice:** Ensure you are pooling Insanity to maximize [Voidform](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=194249) windows.
    - [Shadow Word: Death](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=32379): **(-40.5% Per Cast)** Technical Reason: The reference achieved a 62.5% crit rate and likely timed these better within execute windows compared to your 40% crit rate. **Advice:** Pool charges for the <20% execute phase for maximum value.
