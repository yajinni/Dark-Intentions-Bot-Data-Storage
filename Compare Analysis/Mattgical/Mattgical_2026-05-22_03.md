<small>*(Player: 100.2K DPS | Reference: 127.3K DPS)*</small>

### Encounter: Chimaerus, the Undreamt God (Mythic)
### 1. Audit Conclusions
- **Performance Overview:**
    - Mattgical demonstrates a solid understanding of the Archon Shadow Priest kit, achieving a 51% parse on Mythic Chimaerus. However, there is a significant throughput gap compared to the reference log. The primary drivers for this parse level are suboptimal stat prioritization (significantly lower Haste and Mastery), lower GCD efficiency (287 vs 303 on-GCD casts), and less effective throughput during major cooldown windows.
    - Resource management was excellent with 0 [Insanity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=113) wasted; however, the player relied too heavily on [Mind Flay](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=15407) as a filler rather than maximizing the generation of higher-value spells like [Mind Blast](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=8092) and [Shadow Word: Madness](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=335467).
- **Casting Optimizations:**
    - **[Shadow Word: Madness](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=335467):** Total damage was 15.7% lower than the reference. You missed 5 potential casts over the course of the fight. Ensure this is prioritized as a high-value generator.
    - **[Mind Blast](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=8092):** Total damage was 17% lower. You cast this 6 fewer times than the reference despite a longer fight duration.
    - **[Mind Flay: Insanity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=391403):** Total damage was 21.7% lower. **Per Cast Reasoning:** The 27.6% lower damage per cast is attributed to lower Mastery/Haste scaling and poorer alignment with the [Insidious Ire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=373213) buff (72.2% vs 80.6% uptime). Ensure [Insidious Ire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=373213) stacks are maintained before spending procs.
    - **[Vampiric Touch](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=34914):** Casts were 800% higher (9 vs 1). **Per Cast Reasoning:** The 89.1% value difference is due to the reference's single application benefiting from massive "Pandemic" or talent-based dot extension, whereas your manual refreshes diluted the average per-cast value. **Advice:** Use [Misery](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=238558) to apply dots and avoid manual refreshes unless the target is nearly out of dot uptime.
    - **[Halo](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=120644):** Casts were 16.7% higher. **Per Cast Reasoning:** Your per-cast value was 35% higher than the reference. This is due to your higher raw Intellect and Crit rating (+4.29%) favoring Archon burst windows.
    - **[Mind Flay](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=15407):** Casts were 42.2% higher. **Per Cast Reasoning:** Per cast value was 32.3% lower. **Advice:** You are over-relying on filler. Pool resources to prioritize [Mind Blast](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=8092) and procs over base [Mind Flay](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=15407) channels.
    - **[Tentacle Slam](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1227621):** Total damage 15.8% lower. **Per Cast Reasoning:** Per cast value was 18.9% lower due to lower Mastery and Haste scaling compared to the reference.
    - **[Shadow Word: Pain](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=589):** Total damage 64.2% lower. **Advice:** The reference manually casted this 40 times while you relied entirely on [Vampiric Touch](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=34914) (via Misery) for application. Ensure targets have 100% uptime on both dots to maximize [Psychic Link](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=199486) throughput.
