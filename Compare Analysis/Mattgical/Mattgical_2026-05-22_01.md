       - **[Idol of C'Thun](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=377349):** 25.4% lower damage. **Technical Reason:** This proc is tied to [Mind Flay](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=15407) and [Mind Blast](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=8092) usage. Your lower [Mind Blast](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=8092) count (-9.5%) reduced the frequency of these procs.

### 4. Resource Management (Energy/Combo Points/Mana)

Mattgical shows evidence of inefficient Insanity usage. While cast counts for [Shadow Word: Madness](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=335467) are comparable (50 vs 55), the player's extreme manual casting of [Shadow Word: Pain](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=589) (876 hits) suggests a "panic filler" mentality rather than proper pooling for burst windows. Reference utilizes [Void Bolt](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1264177) much more effectively to generate Insanity and maintain DoTs, allowing them to focus GCDs on higher-value spells.
