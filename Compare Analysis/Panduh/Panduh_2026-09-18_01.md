### 1. Audit Conclusions
- **Performance Overview:**
    - The player's performance was significantly below the reference, largely due to major gaps in core spell scaling and cooldown management. While the player maintained decent uptime on core mechanics, the per-cast damage values indicate a failure to maximize the potency of their rotational windows.
    - The most critical factors limiting the parse were suboptimal use of [Touch of the Magi](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=210833) and lower-than-average [Arcane Missiles](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=7268) output. Resource management was stable, but the lack of damage density during peak windows suggests ineffective cooldown alignment.
- **Casting Optimizations:**
    - [Touch of the Magi](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=210833): Player 6 casts (1.27 CPM), Reference 7 casts (1.41 CPM), Player Total 3.18M (-50.2%), Per-cast 529.4K (-44.5%)
        - **Advice:** The significant loss in total and per-cast damage indicates poor alignment with secondary cooldowns or target uptime issues. Ensure [Touch of the Magi](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=210833) is activated immediately before your highest-damage fillers to capitalize on the stored damage explosion.
    - [Prismatic Bolt](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1295924): Player 26 casts (5.5 CPM), Reference 26 casts (5.25 CPM), Player Total 10.66M (-43.0%), Per-cast 409.9K (-45.5%)
        - **Advice:** Despite similar cast counts, the per-cast damage is vastly lower. This suggests you are casting [Prismatic Bolt](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1295924) outside of optimal debuff windows or with fewer stacks of damage-boosting buffs.
    - [Arcane Missiles](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=7268): Player 71 casts (15.01 CPM), Reference 76 casts (15.34 CPM), Player Total 17.00M (-40.6%), Per-cast 239.5K (-39.3%)
        - **Advice:** Your lower cast count and lower damage per cast suggest [Clearcasting](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=263725) procs are not being consumed efficiently. Prioritize these procs to maximize throughput.
    - [Arcane Barrage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=44425): Player 69 casts (14.59 CPM), Reference 66 casts (13.32 CPM), Player Total 10.91M (-34.6%), Per-cast 158.1K (-40.3%)
        - **Advice:** You are over-casting [Arcane Barrage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=44425) relative to the reference. Be careful not to expend charges prematurely; reserve it for high-charge thresholds to avoid losing damage from your mastery-based burn phase.
