# Seze (43% DPS Parse) vs. Gerónimus (99% DPS Parse)
<small>*(Player: 94.3K DPS | Reference: 133.1K DPS)*</small>

### Encounter: Chimaerus, the Undreamt God (Mythic)

### 1. Audit Conclusions
- **Performance Overview:**  
  Seze's performance resulted in a 43% parse, primarily due to severe rotational gaps, lower overall spell cast density, and major cooldown misalignment compared to Gerónimus. While Seze's resource management showed a lower waste percentage of Rage (3.2% vs. 7.6%), this is actually a symptom of playing too passively—Seze generated significantly less total Rage overall (4,153 vs. 5,395), indicating a slow casting tempo, downtime, and a failure to aggressively roll global cooldowns (GCDs). This directly reduced the uptime of [Enrage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184362) to 87.5% (compared to 94.9% for the reference) and led to a massive loss in overall DPS.
  
- **Casting Optimizations:**  
  *   **[Rampage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184367)**: Seze casted 17.3% fewer Rampages (105 vs. 127). Ensure you cast [Rampage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184367) as soon as you hit 80+ Rage to maintain high [Enrage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184362) uptime and avoid pooling resource to the 100-Rage cap.
  *   **[Bladestorm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=46924)**: Seze casted 7 Bladestorms compared to 9 for the reference, and suffered a **41.3% loss in damage per cast** (491.1K vs. 837.0K). This massive per-cast deficit occurred because Seze frequently casted Bladestorm without aligning it with major damage multipliers like [Enrage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184362) or during low target density phases. Ensure Bladestorm is only initiated while fully Enraged, ideally when high-priority add packs (such as Swarming Shades) are grouped.
  *   **[Execute](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=5308)**: Seze casted 35.5% fewer Executes (40 vs. 62) and saw a **12.4% lower per-cast value** (79.2K vs. 90.4K). This deficit stems from lower Critical Strike scaling (9.00% vs. 10.96%), lower primary stat scaling, and casting Execute outside of [Enrage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184362) and [Executioner's Wrath](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1265575) windows. Prioritize Execute on cooldown over core generators like Raging Blow.
  *   **[Odyn's Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=385060)**: Casts were 20.0% lower (12 vs. 15). Use Odyn's Fury strictly on its 45s cooldown and align it with your Recklessness burst windows.
  *   **[Bloodbath](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=335096)**: Seze casted 26.0% fewer Bloodbaths (37 vs. 50). Ensure you do not delay your core bloodthirst/bloodbath priorities, which are vital for Rage generation.
  *   **[Bloodthirst](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=23881)**: Seze had 16.4% fewer casts (51 vs. 61), representing idle global cooldowns.
  *   **[Raging Blow](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=85288)**: Seze casted 24.4% fewer Raging Blows (34 vs. 45). Keep generators active whenever spenders or Execute are not available.
  *   **[Whirlwind](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=190411)**: Seze over-casted Whirlwind by 46.9% (47 vs. 32) but suffered a **36.1% drop in per-cast damage** (24.0K vs. 37.6K). This is a rotational error; Seze spammed Whirlwind unnecessarily—likely on single targets or outside of high-value add cleave phases. Use Whirlwind strictly to maintain the cleave buff on multi-target encounters and avoid wasting valuable GCDs.
  *   **[Wrecking Throw](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=394354)**: Seze casted this 5 times compared to 2 for the reference, seeing a **63.4% higher per-cast value** (167.4K vs. 102.4K). This indicates Seze successfully utilized the ability when mechanical absorb shields were active, though care must be taken not to delay the primary rotation unless necessary.
  *   **[Melee](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1)**: Seze had 21.0% fewer melee swings (377 vs. 477), pointing directly to downtime or being out of melee range of the boss during movement mechanics.
  *   **[Rend](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=772)**: Seze had 0 manual casts but registered passive hits, whereas the reference casted it once manually. This represents a functional 100% cast difference. Ensure passive application pathways are prioritized over manual casts.

**Summary Stats:**
* **Seze Total Recklessness Damage:** 14.28M (52.9%)
* **Gerónimus Total Recklessness Damage:** 25.96M (55.2%)

- **Timings and Alignment:**  
  While both players managed 8 total casts of [Recklessness](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1719), Seze's throughput during these windows was heavily compromised. Specifically, Cast 2 (941.5K vs. 5.47M), Cast 5 (2.05M vs. 5.56M), Cast 6 (265.5K vs. 2.41M), and Cast 8 (371.8K vs. 2.46M) show severe damage deficits. Gerónimus meticulously aligned their Recklessness windows with the spawn of heavy add phases (like Swarming Shades and Colossal Horrors) and ensured [Enrage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184362) was active *before* entering the window, unlocking massive cleave damage. Seze deployed the cooldowns blindly without establishing mechanical or target-density alignment.

- **Throughput Efficiency:**  
  Seze's lower damage inside Recklessness was a direct consequence of empty GCDs, lack of resource pooling, and rotational errors. Spamming [Whirlwind](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=190411) during these high-priority burst phases instead of pooling Rage for [Rampage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184367) and executing [Bladestorm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=46924) directly tanked Seze's burst potential.
