### Encounter: Chimaerus, the Undreamt God (Mythic)
### 1. Audit Conclusions
- **Performance Overview:**
    Seze's performance is significantly below the reference, primarily due to a lack of rotational aggression and poor maintenance of the spec's core engine. While the player demonstrated good consumable usage and proactive defensive utility, the fundamental throughput was hampered by a 17.3% deficit in [Rampage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184367) casts, leading to a sub-optimal [Enrage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184362) uptime (87.5% vs. 94.9%). Furthermore, a massive 35.5% gap in [Execute](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=5308) casts indicates missed opportunities to capitalize on the Slayer hero tree's execute-centric resets.
- **Casting Optimizations:**
    - [Rampage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184367): Seze recorded 17.3% fewer casts. **Advice:** This is your highest priority spender. You must pool rage and cast this immediately at 80+ Rage to maintain 100% [Enrage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184362) uptime.
    - [Bladestorm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=46924): Seze had 22.2% fewer casts and a 41.3% lower per-cast value. **Technical Reason:** The significantly lower per-cast damage (491.1K vs 837.0K) stems from casting [Bladestorm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=46924) outside of [Recklessness](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1719) windows or while not [Enraged](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184362). **Advice:** Ensure you are always [Enraged](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184362) before starting the channel.
    - [Execute](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=5308): 35.5% fewer casts and a 12.4% lower per-cast value. **Technical Reason:** The lower per-cast damage is due to lower crit rates and missing stat alignment during the execute phase. **Advice:** Use [Execute](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=5308) on cooldown whenever available; as a Slayer, this is vital for throughput and resets.
    - [Odyn's Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=385060): 20.0% fewer casts. **Advice:** Use this massive burst tool on cooldown, ideally aligned with [Recklessness](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1719).
    - [Bloodbath](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=335096): 26.0% fewer casts. **Advice:** Prioritize this as your main generator when [Enraged](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184362).
    - [Bloodthirst](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=23881): 16.4% fewer casts. **Advice:** Increase casting frequency to generate Rage when higher priority spenders are not available.
    - [Raging Blow](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=85288): 24.4% fewer casts. **Advice:** Do not let this generator sit on maximum charges.
    - [Whirlwind](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=190411): 46.9% more casts but 36.1% lower per-cast value. **Advice:** You are over-relying on [Whirlwind](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=190411) as a filler. Only use it when required for cleave or when all higher-priority generators/spenders are on cooldown.
    - [Wrecking Throw](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=394354): 150.0% more casts. **Advice:** This is very situational; ensure it doesn't displace core rotational GCDs.
    - [Impending Victory](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=202168): 100.0% more casts. **Advice:** While useful for self-healing, using it too frequently when at high health wastes a GCD that could be used for damage.
