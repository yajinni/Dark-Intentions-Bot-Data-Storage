# Seze (43% DPS Parse) vs. Gerónimus (99% DPS Parse)
<small>*(Player: 94.3K DPS | Reference: 133.1K DPS)*</small>

### Encounter: Chimaerus, the Undreamt God (Mythic)
### 1. Audit Conclusions
- **Performance Overview:**
    Seze demonstrates a functional understanding of the Fury Warrior priority system but suffers from a significant deficit in rotational tempo and cooldown alignment. The primary driver of the 43% parse is the lower [Enrage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184362) uptime (87.5% vs. 94.9%) and a substantial under-utilization of [Execute](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=5308). Resource management was relatively stable, though the player generated significantly less total Rage than the reference, indicating lower GCD efficiency and missed generator casts.
- **Casting Optimizations:**
    - **[Execute](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=5308):** Seze cast 40 times compared to Gerónimus's 62. As a Slayer Warrior, [Execute](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=5308) is your most vital rotational priority and reset engine. You are missing nearly 35% of your available Execute windows. **Advice:** Prioritize [Execute](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=5308) on cooldown and immediately upon [Sudden Death](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=52437) procs.
    - **[Rampage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184367):** 17.3% fewer casts than the reference. This directly correlates to your lower [Enrage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184362) uptime. **Advice:** Follow the "80-Rage Rule"—spend Rage on [Rampage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184367) as soon as you hit 80+ Rage to ensure you never cap and to refresh [Enrage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184362) proactively.
    - **[Bladestorm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=46924):** The player's per-cast damage was 41.3% lower than the reference. **Advice:** This indicates you are casting [Bladestorm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=46924) without being [Enraged](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184362) or without [Recklessness](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1719) active. Always ensure you are Enraged before entering a Bladestorm window to benefit from the damage mastery bonus.
    - **[Whirlwind](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=190411):** The player cast this 46.9% more often than the reference, but with 36.1% lower per-cast value. **Advice:** You are likely using [Whirlwind](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=190411) as a filler too frequently. Prioritize [Raging Blow](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=85288) or [Bloodthirst](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=23881) for Rage generation over Whirlwind in single-target scenarios unless you need to cleave.
    - **[Bloodthirst](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=23881):** 16.4% fewer casts. **Advice:** This is a core Rage generator; ensure you are not leaving gaps in your GCD rotation where a generator could have been used.
