### Encounter: Chimaerus, the Undreamt God (Mythic)
### 1. Audit Conclusions
- **Performance Overview:**
    - The player's performance was hindered by significantly lower rotational execution and poor cooldown alignment. 
    - The primary issue is a lack of priority for **Execute** and inefficient **Bladestorm** usage, leading to a massive damage gap in key burst windows.
    - While resource management (Section 5) showed only 3.2% rage waste, the frequency of spending (casts per minute) was significantly lower than the reference, suggesting a lack of global uptime and slower reaction to procs.
- **Casting Optimizations:**
    - **Bladestorm**: 22.2% fewer casts and 41.3% lower damage. Advice: Ensure Bladestorm is used on cooldown during *Recklessness* windows; the reference used this significantly more aggressively to capture priority target cleave.
    - **Execute**: 35.5% fewer casts and 100% loss in specific sub-spell damage. Advice: Prioritize [Execute](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=5308) immediately upon proc or when the target is below 20%. The player missed numerous windows to burn rage.
    - **Whirlwind**: 46.9% more casts but 36.1% less damage. Advice: The player is over-relying on [Whirlwind](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=190411) as a filler. Stop using it as a primary rotational tool unless you need to proc *Meat Cleaver*; prioritize [Bloodthirst](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=23881) or [Raging Blow](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=85288) to maintain *Enrage*.
    - **Charge**: 30% more casts but 100% loss in specific sub-spell damage. Advice: Avoid excessive [Charge](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100) usage if it is not generating the intended rage or uptime; focus on remaining in melee range.
