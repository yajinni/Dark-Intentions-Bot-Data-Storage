# Seze (43% DPS Parse) vs. Gerónimus (99% DPS Parse)
<small>*(Player: 94.3K DPS | Reference: 133.1K DPS)*</small>

### Encounter: Chimaerus, the Undreamt God (Mythic)

### 1. Audit Conclusions
- **Performance Overview:**
    Seze performed at a 43% parse level, demonstrating a solid understanding of basic mechanical defensive positioning but showing substantial rotational inefficiencies. The primary driver of the performance gap is a lack of rotational urgency, leading to 64 fewer active GCDs compared to the reference. This pacing deficit, combined with key spell delays, caused a significant drop in Rage generation and a -7.4% deficit in critical **Enrage** uptime.
- **Casting Optimizations:**
    *   **[Rampage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184367)**: Seze had 17.3% fewer casts of Rampage, resulting in a 24.6% reduction in damage. This directly impacted Enrage uptime (87.5% vs 94.9%). **Advice:** Pool Rage efficiently and prioritize Rampage immediately upon reaching 80+ Rage to ensure maximum Enrage coverage.
    *   **[Bladestorm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=46924)**: Seze cast Bladestorm 22.2% fewer times, resulting in a 54.3% loss in total damage. Crucially, the player's damage per cast was 41.3% lower (491.1K vs 837.0K). **Advice:** This significant per-cast discrepancy indicates Bladestorm was cast without crucial damage modifiers or was cancelled early. Always ensure you are Enraged before entering a Bladestorm channel, and align it with major Slayer spec multipliers.
    *   **[Execute](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=5308)**: Seze missed 22 potential Execute casts (-35.5% difference). **Advice:** Execute is your highest-priority builder and execution tool. Ensure you never delay Execute for standard builders like Raging Blow or Bloodthirst when it is active.
    *   **[Odyn's Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=385060)**: Casts were 20.0% lower. **Advice:** Do not delay Odyn's Fury. Keep this high-intensity burst bleed on cooldown, ideally aligning it with your Recklessness windows.
    *   **[Bloodbath](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=335096)**: Casts were 26.0% lower. **Advice:** This is a symptom of delayed active rotational pacing. Focus on maintaining a tight spell queue to maximize empowered Bloodthirst/Bloodbath procs.
    *   **[Bloodthirst](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=23881)**: Seze cast this 16.4% less often. **Advice:** Minimize empty gaps in your rotation to keep your basic Rage-generating engine running.
    *   **[Raging Blow](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=85288)**: Seze had 24.4% fewer casts, primarily due to rotational drifting. **Advice:** Maintain a higher overall cast pacing.
    *   **[Whirlwind](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=190411)**: Seze cast Whirlwind 46.9% more often, but suffered a 36.1% lower per-cast damage average (24.0K vs 37.6K). **Advice:** Whirlwind is a filler and a mechanical enabler for cleave (Meat Cleaver). Seze spammed it excessively as a filler, wasting high-priority GCDs. Only cast Whirlwind when necessary to spread your single-target attacks.
    *   **[Wrecking Throw](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=394354)**: Cast 150.0% more often. **Advice:** Wrecking Throw is highly situational. Do not waste active GCDs using it as a standard rotational filler.
