# Seze (43% DPS Parse) vs. Gerónimus (99% DPS Parse)
<small>*(Player: 94.3K DPS | Reference: 133.1K DPS)*</small>

### Encounter: Chimaerus, the Undreamt God (Mythic)
### 1. Audit Conclusions
- **Performance Overview:**
    - Seze performed significantly below the reference level, primarily due to a lower activity rate (Total Casts: 380 vs 444) and critical failures in maintaining core spec mechanics.
    - The most significant rotational issue was the failure to maintain 100% [Enrage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184362) uptime (87.5% vs 94.9%), which is the engine of the Fury Warrior.
    - Resource management (Section 5) shows that while overcapping was low, the player generated significantly less Rage overall, leading to fewer [Rampage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184367) and [Execute](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=5308) casts.
- **Casting Optimizations:**
    - **[Execute](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=5308):** Seze cast Execute 35.5% fewer times than the reference. The per-cast value was 12.4% lower (79.2K vs 90.4K), largely driven by a lower crit rate (35.6% vs 42.4%). Ensure [Execute](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=5308) is used on cooldown as the highest priority generator/spender.
    - **[Bladestorm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=46924):** Seze had 22.2% fewer casts and a massive 41.3% lower per-cast value. This technical difference is likely due to timing; [Bladestorm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=46924) must be aligned with high-value [Enrage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184362) and [Recklessness](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1719) windows to maximize the Slayer hero spec benefits.
    - **[Rampage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184367):** 17.3% fewer casts. As the primary Rage spender, missing these casts directly lowers [Enrage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184362) uptime and overall throughput. Pool Rage and spend immediately at 80+ to avoid capping.
    - **[Bloodthirst](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=23881):** 16.4% fewer casts. This reduces Rage generation and the frequency of [Bloodbath](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=335096) procs.
    - **[Whirlwind](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=190411):** Seze cast this 46.9% more often than the reference but saw a 36.1% lower average per cast. This indicates over-reliance on [Whirlwind](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=190411) as a filler when higher priority spells like [Raging Blow](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=85288) or [Execute](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=5308) should have been prioritized.
    - **[Melee](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1):** 21.0% fewer hits. This points to significant target uptime issues or excessive downtime during the encounter.
