### 1. Audit Conclusions
   - **Performance Overview:**
       - Eatsboogers demonstrated a solid understanding of the core Unholy rotation but suffered from significant resource management issues and poor cooldown alignment. While the player maintained decent uptime on core buffs like [Icy Talons](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=194879), the massive gap in Runic Power efficiency (25.9% waste) and missed [Soul Reaper](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=343294) casts in the execute window severely hampered their overall throughput.
       - The player's performance was further hindered by a significant stat disparity; the reference player prioritized Mastery (27.4%) over Critical Strike (18.3%), whereas Eatsboogers favored Critical Strike (30.1%), leading to much lower scaling on Shadow damage abilities like [Putrefy](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1247378).
   - **Casting Optimizations:**
       - [Putrefy](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1247378): **Casts: +85.7% | Value: -43.8%**. Despite casting this significantly more, the per-cast damage was 43.8% lower (346.1K vs 615.8K). Technical Reason: [Putrefy](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1247378) scales with Shadow damage; your lower Mastery (15.35% vs 27.43%) significantly reduces its impact. Advice: Re-prioritize Mastery on gear and ensure [Dark Transformation](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1233448) is active to maximize this ability's value.
       - [Graveyard](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=383269): **Casts: -52.6% | Value: -71.7%**. You managed only 9 casts compared to the reference's 19. Technical Reason: Missing nearly half of the potential [Graveyard](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=383269) procs suggests poor management of the Rider of the Apocalypse mechanic. Advice: Ensure you are spending [Festering Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=85948) to build Rider stacks more aggressively to summon Horsemen consistently.
       - [Death Coil](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=47632): **Casts: -14.8% | Value: -49.4%**. Per-cast damage was 49.4% lower (23.6K vs 46.6K). Technical Reason: The reference utilized [Coil Of Devastation](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=390270), providing a significant damage boost you lacked. Advice: Review your talent choice in the Unholy tree; [Coil Of Devastation](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=390270) is vital for high-end Death Coil throughput.
       - [Soul Reaper](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=343294): **Casts: -31.3%**. You missed roughly 5 casts during the execute window. Advice: [Soul Reaper](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=343294) is your highest priority whenever the target is below 35% HP. Track this window more closely.
       - [Necrotic Coil](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1242174): **Casts: -15.2%**. This indicates fewer [Sudden Doom](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=81340) procs utilized or lower Runic Power generation. Advice: Focus on reducing Runic Power waste (currently 25.9%) to ensure you have the resources to cast [Necrotic Coil](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1242174) whenever available.
       - [Scourge Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=55090): **Value: -18.5%**. Technical Reason: Lower Mastery scaling. Advice: In the Rider build, ensure you have 3+ Lesser Ghoul stacks before using [Scourge Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=55090) to maximize efficiency.
       - [Epidemic](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=207317): **Value: -26.0%**. Per-cast damage was significantly lower. Advice: Use [Epidemic](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=207317) only when target density is high enough to outweigh the single-target value of [Death Coil](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=47632).
       - [Festering Scythe](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=458128): **Value: -17.3%**. Technical Reason: Missing Mastery scaling and [Blighted](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1271199) buff alignment.
       - [Death and Decay](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=43265): **Casts: +100%**. You used this more frequently for AoE, but the reference prioritized single-target spending. Advice: Ensure [Death and Decay](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=43265) is placed specifically for Cleave windows and not just as a filler.
