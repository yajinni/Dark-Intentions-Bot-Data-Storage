### 1. Audit Conclusions
   - **Performance Overview:**
       - Eatsboogers demonstrated a solid understanding of the Unholy Death Knight core, achieving a respectable 84th percentile parse. However, a significant 26K DPS gap exists compared to the reference. This discrepancy is primarily driven by critical Runic Power management issues, where the player wasted 255 RP compared to the reference's 3. Furthermore, while the player's rune spending discipline was mathematically higher, the "Per Cast" value of almost all shadow-based abilities was significantly lower due to a 6.47% Mastery deficit and missing talent synergies like [Coil of Devastation](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=390270).
   - **Casting Optimizations:**
       - **[Putrefy](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1247378):** (Casts: 11 vs 5 | Per Cast: 216.2K vs 443.5K). The player cast this more frequently but with 51.3% lower efficiency. The reference likely aligned these casts more strictly with [Dark Transformation](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1233448) and higher Mastery scaling. **Advice:** Ensure [Dark Transformation](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1233448) is active and the target is above 35% HP to maximize [Putrefy](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1247378) value.
       - **[Necrotic Coil](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1242174):** (Casts: 16 vs 27 | Per Cast: 58.4K vs 70.8K). Lower cast count and 17.5% lower value. This indicates lower [Sudden Doom](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=81340) proc utilization and lower Mastery. **Advice:** Prioritize [Necrotic Coil](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1242174) to spend RP and avoid capping, which also feeds into [Army of the Damned](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1242294) cooldown reduction.
       - **[Death Coil](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=47632):** (Casts: 27 vs 36 | Per Cast: 20.4K vs 44.5K). Significant 54.2% value difference. The reference utilized [Coil of Devastation](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=390270), which the player lacked. **Advice:** Align your talent build with the reference to include [Coil of Devastation](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=390270) for higher RP spender throughput.
       - **[Melee](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1):** (Casts: 89 vs 302). Massive cast difference (70.5%). This suggests significant downtime away from the boss or lower Haste (3.45% difference). **Advice:** Improve uptime on the boss; as a Rider, your [Death Charge](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=444347) should minimize travel time.
       - **[Graveyard](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=383269):** (Casts: 7 vs 5 | Per Cast: 51.5K vs 194.9K). Despite more casts, the player's [Graveyard](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=383269) damage was 73.6% lower per event. **Advice:** This ability's scaling is heavily tied to active buffs and Mastery. Ensure it is used within the full burst window of [Army of the Dead](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=42650).
       - **[Soul Reaper](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=343294):** (Casts: 7 vs 9). Missed execute opportunities. **Advice:** Strictly use on cooldown once the target hits < 35% HP to trigger the secondary explosion.
       - **[Scourge Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=55090):** (Casts: 72 vs 81 | Per Cast: 18.9K vs 22.4K). 15.5% lower value. **Advice:** Ensure you have 2-4 Festering Wounds and at least 3 Lesser Ghoul stacks before spending with [Scourge Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=55090) to maximize Rider efficiency.
       - **[Death and Decay](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=43265):** (Casts: 8 vs 0). The player used this in a single-target/low-density environment where the reference skipped it. **Advice:** In pure single-target, [Death and Decay](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=43265) is a lower priority unless cleaving [Scourge Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=55090).
