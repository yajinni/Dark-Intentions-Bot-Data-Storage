### Encounter: Chimaerus, the Undreamt God (Heroic)

### 1. Audit Conclusions
   - **Performance Overview:**
       - Tinytoebeans demonstrated a solid understanding of the Archon Holy Priest toolkit, maintaining high uptime on core buffs and utilizing the [Spiritwell](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=453112) synergy effectively. However, the throughput gap is primarily driven by significant differences in secondary stat scaling (Crit/Mastery) and a lack of [Flash Heal](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2061) usage to maintain [Lightweaver](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=390632) or bridge gaps in low-intensity healing phases.
       - Resource management was efficient, but throughput suffered from lower [Intellect](https://www.wowhead.com/rating=Intellect) and [Crit](https://www.wowhead.com/rating=Crit) ratings, resulting in lower per-cast values across nearly all primary spells.
   - **Casting Optimizations:**
       - **[Flash Heal](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2061):** Casts were 77.4% lower than the reference. While Archon prioritizes [Prayer of Healing](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=596) during [Surge of Light](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=114255) procs, you must still use Flash Heal to maintain triage efficiency and stack [Lightweaver](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=390632) if talented.
       - **[Holy Word: Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2050):** Per-cast value was 30.8% lower. This is a direct result of significantly lower Intellect and Crit scaling. Ensure you are maximizing [Intellect](https://www.wowhead.com/rating=Intellect) through proper consumables and gear.
       - **[Halo](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=120517):** Casts were 16.7% lower. As an Archon, [Halo](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=120517) is a rotational anchor that triggers powerful Hero Talent procs. Missing even one cast results in a significant throughput loss. Use it strictly on cooldown.
       - **[Prayer of Healing](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=596):** Value was 15.5% lower per cast. Beyond stat differences, ensure PoH is cast primarily when [Surge of Light](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=114255) is active to benefit from Archon's [Spiritwell](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=453112) buffs.
       - **[Benediction](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1262763):** Value was 53.3% lower despite having 28.6% more casts. This suggests a large amount of the healing was lost to overhealing or failed to tick fully. Monitor target health before application.
       - **[Divine Hymn](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=64844):** Total healing was 31.3% lower. This is likely due to the lack of Intellect and Mastery scaling compared to the reference.
       - **[Holy Nova](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=281265):** You cast this 4 times while the reference cast it 0. [Holy Nova](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=281265) is extremely inefficient in raid scenarios; these GCDs are better spent on [Smite](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=585) fillers or preparing for burst.

### 4. Resource Management
Analysis of combat data shows that Tinytoebeans managed mana well, but missed opportunities for **Serendipity** resets. 
- **Holy Word CDR:** The lack of [Smite](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=585) casts during periods of low damage meant slower resets on [Holy Word: Serenity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2050). 
- **Surge of Light:** Uptime on [Surge of Light](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=114255) was consistent, but the Archon priority is to use these procs on [Prayer of Healing](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=596) for [Spiritwell](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=453112) benefits. While PoH cast counts were high, the per-cast value shows these weren't always aligned with high-damage windows.
